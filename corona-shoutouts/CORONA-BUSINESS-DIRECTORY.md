## 📊 Directory Structure

Each business listing will include:

```markdown
### Business Name
**Category:** [Primary category]
**Address:** [Street address, Corona, CA ZIP]
**Phone:** [Phone number]
**Website:** [URL]
**Hours:** [Operating hours]
**Social Media:** Instagram, Facebook, etc.

**Description:** [2-3 sentence overview]

**Specialties:**
- [Key offering 1]
- [Key offering 2]
- [Key offering 3]

**Why We Love Them:** [Corona Shoutouts editorial note]

**Featured In:** [Links to newsletter mentions]
```
