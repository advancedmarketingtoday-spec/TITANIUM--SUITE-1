# Why We're Building This: A Letter to Corona

Let's be completely honest for a second.

In a world full of digital noise, endless scrolling, and algorithms designed to keep us divided or stressed, it is easy to forget what a local community is actually supposed to feel like. A town isn't just a collection of ZIP codes, traffic lights, and strip malls. It's the people. It's the familiar faces at the local coffee shop, the family-owned restaurant that's been serving Dos Lagos for years, the high school games under Friday night lights, and the quiet pride we share in calling Corona home.

That is why we are here, and that is why this project exists.

Everything we write, everything we spotlight, and every story we share is built on three simple promises to you:

**Absolute Honesty:** No fluff, no hidden angles, and no corporate spin. Just real, authentic talk about what's happening right here in our backyard. If a place is great, we want you to know why. If there's something worth celebrating, we're going to shout it from the rooftops.

**Genuine Care for the People:** This isn't about chasing empty clicks or viral stunts. It's about looking out for our neighbors. We want to lift up the local business owners who pour their hearts into their work, highlight the hidden gems making a difference, and celebrate the everyday magic of living in Corona.

**Bringing Joy, Love, and Value:** At the end of the day, when you open up what we write, the goal is simple: we want you to smile. We want to bring a little bit of joy and warmth into your day, give you the answers and resources you're actually looking for, and flood this community with a whole lot of love.

You deserve a local voice that feels like a conversation with a trusted friend—someone who genuinely cares about this city and the people who make it special.

We're just getting started, and we are so glad you're here for the journey. Here's to bringing a little more joy, connection, and heart to Corona, together.

---

## Editorial Compass - The Three Promises

Every article, every story, every spotlight must deliver on:

1. **Absolute Honesty** - Real, authentic, no spin
2. **Genuine Care for the People** - Lift up neighbors, celebrate community
3. **Bringing Joy, Love, and Value** - Make readers smile

**North Star Question:** "Will this make a Corona resident smile and feel good about their community?"

If no, don't publish it.
