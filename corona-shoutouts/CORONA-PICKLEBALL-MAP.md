## 🎾 Corona Pickleball Locations

### Public Parks with Pickleball Courts

#### 1. **Corona Heritage Park & Senior Center**
- **Address:** 3850 Railroad St, Corona, CA 92880
- **Courts:** 8 dedicated pickleball courts
- **Type:** Dedicated outdoor courts
- **Lighting:** Yes (for evening play)
- **Parking:** Ample free parking
- **Features:**
  - Adjacent to Senior Center
  - Restrooms available
  - Water fountains
  - Shaded seating areas
- **Schedule:** Open during park hours
- **Popular Times:** Early mornings (7-9 AM), evenings (5-7 PM)
- **Skill Level:** All levels welcome
- **Drop-in Play:** Yes, weekday mornings

**Notes:** This is Corona's premier pickleball destination with the most courts.

## 🌡️ Best Times to Play (Weather)

### **Fall/Winter (Oct-Mar):**
- ✅ **Mid-day:** 10 AM - 3 PM (comfortable)
- ⚠️ **Mornings:** Can be chilly
- ✅ **Evenings:** Cool but playable (if lit)

### **Spring (Apr-May):**
- ✅ **Mornings:** Perfect 7-10 AM
- ✅ **Evenings:** Ideal 5-8 PM
- ⚠️ **Mid-day:** Getting warm

### **Summer (Jun-Sep):**
- ✅ **Early Morning:** 6-9 AM (best)
- ❌ **Mid-day:** Too hot (90-105°F)
- ✅ **Evening:** 6-9 PM (if lit courts)

**Pro Tip:** Corona summers are HOT. Play early or late with plenty of water.
