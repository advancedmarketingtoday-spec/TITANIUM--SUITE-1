# Dos Lagos - Corona's Premier Lifestyle Destination

**Location:** 2780 Cabot Drive, Corona, CA 92883  
**Type:** Mixed-use development (shopping, dining, golf, residential)  
**Management:** Simon Property Group

---

## Overview

Dos Lagos is Corona's upscale lifestyle center combining shopping, dining, entertainment, golf, and lakeside ambiance. Located in the heart of Corona, it's a community gathering place and destination for both locals and visitors.
