Whether you're a serious golfer or haven't swung a club since high school, The Links is worth checking out:
- Lakeside holes with water features
- Full driving range and practice greens
- Pro shop with gear and lessons
- Clubhouse restaurant and bar
- Tournaments, leagues, and events
- **Bonus:** It's also a wedding and event venue

Even if you don't golf, the clubhouse is a great spot for lunch with a view.
