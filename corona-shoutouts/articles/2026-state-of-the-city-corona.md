# 2026 State of the City in Corona, California: What to Know

Corona residents have an opportunity to hear directly about the city's progress, current projects and vision for the future at the **2026 State of the City** on Thursday, October 8, 2026.

The City of Corona lists this year's theme as **"Grand by Design."** The event will take place at the Historic Civic Center Theater, 815 W. 6th St., Corona, CA 92882.

## Event schedule

- **6:00 PM:** Pre-show entertainment
- **6:30 PM:** State of the City Address
- **7:00 PM:** Social reception

Admission is free, but the City says space is limited and asks attendees to RSVP.

## Why Corona residents may want to attend

The annual State of the City is a useful opportunity to hear how Corona describes its progress, projects and plans for the community. Residents interested in local development, infrastructure, city services and Corona's future can hear the presentation in person and connect with other community members at the reception.

## Before you go

Because event details can change, confirm the latest information and RSVP instructions on the City of Corona's official event page before leaving.
