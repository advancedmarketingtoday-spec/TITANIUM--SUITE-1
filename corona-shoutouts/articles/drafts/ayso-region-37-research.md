## Published parent information

The official FAQ's indexed 2026 information lists:

| Program | Listed fee |
| --- | ---: |
| 6U–19U | $195 |
| 4U/5U academies | $90 |
| EPIC | $70 |

The FAQ says registration includes the $25 national fee and a jersey, shorts, and socks. Other team or tournament costs may apply. Shin guards are required; cleats and an age-appropriate ball are recommended.

Published field assignments place younger core divisions at Shearer Park and 10U–14U at Santana Park. EPIC is listed for Saturday mornings at Santana. Older divisions may travel. Practice arrangements depend on the coach.

These are published listings, not confirmation of current spaces, final checkout totals, or an individual team's schedule. Direct organizer confirmation remains outstanding.
