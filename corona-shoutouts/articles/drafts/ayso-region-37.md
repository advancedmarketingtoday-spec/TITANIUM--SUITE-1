## Article draft

# How AYSO Region 37 Brings Corona Families Together

**Youth soccer, encouraging coaches, and local volunteers give Corona and Norco families a place to connect.**

For a child learning soccer, a small moment can feel like a big win: making a first pass, trying a new skill, or hearing encouragement after a missed shot.

AYSO Region 37 serves Corona and Norco families through a volunteer-run soccer program. Its focus includes helping players develop skills, enjoy the game, and learn how to be good teammates.

## A Chance to Play and Grow

AYSO’s “Everyone Plays” philosophy means each player participates in at least half of every game. Positive coaching and good sportsmanship are also central to the program. The goal is to give children opportunities to learn through playing, with encouragement along the way.

## Local Volunteers Make It Possible

Region 37 relies on volunteers, including coaches, referees, assistant coaches, and team managers. Parents and other adults who want to help can explore volunteer opportunities through the region’s website.

For families, volunteering offers a practical way to support youth sports and become more involved in the community.

## Soccer for Players of Different Abilities

Region 37 also lists an EPIC program for athletes with disabilities. EPIC stands for “Everyone Plays In our Community” and was formerly known as VIP. The program provides additional support so athletes can participate in soccer.

## What Parents Should Know

Region 37’s published 2026 fall information lists a season running from early August through mid-November, with programs including 4U and 5U academies, 6U–19U divisions, and EPIC.

Interested in joining? Start with the [official Region 37 registration page](https://www.ayso37.org/Default.aspx?tabid=943429). Confirm:

- Whether your child’s division is accepting players or has a waiting list.
- The current total cost and what registration includes.
- Practice and game locations.
- Required equipment and age eligibility.

Because the fall season has already started, check availability directly before making plans.
