# Five Coffee Shops to Try in Corona

*From a Sixth Street coffee stop to a café tucked inside a Dos Lagos boutique, these local options give you a few reasons to change your usual order.*

A coffee run can fit into ten spare minutes, or it can be the reason to finally catch up with a friend. Choosing a place gets easier when you know what makes each one different.

These five Corona shops offer several starting points: specialty drinks, a place to sit, breakfast with your coffee, or some browsing while you’re out. This is an editorial shortlist, not a scored ranking or a claim that we’ve personally taste-tested every café in town. Menus and hours can change, so check with the shop before making a special trip.

## Three Ring Coffee: a stop on Sixth Street

Three Ring Coffee is listed at **301 W. Sixth Street**, with local directories identifying Suite 104. For anyone spending time in central Corona, its location makes it an easy shop to put on the list.

Customer reviews collected by Roadtrippers mention espresso, pastries, and a drink called Foam Top. Those are useful leads when deciding what to ask about at the counter, though the current menu should guide your order. If you usually choose the same latte everywhere, ask what the staff recommends that day.

Online listings disagree about some opening days. Check before heading over, especially if you’re planning a weekday visit. [Location and customer reviews](https://maps.roadtrippers.com/us/corona-ca/food-drink/three-ring-coffee-corona-ca--0).

## Canvas Coffee: an option for sitting down

Canvas Coffee sits at **2331 Kellogg Avenue**. Explore Corona lists outdoor seating and free Wi-Fi among its amenities, while customer-review summaries describe natural light and room to sit.

That makes Canvas a candidate for a coffee catch-up or a little time with a book. If you need a place to work, check the available seating and any laptop guidelines when you arrive; no online listing can promise you a quiet table.

The shop’s own social-link page is a useful starting point for updates before a visit. [Canvas Coffee’s official links](https://linktr.ee/canvascoffeeca).

## Miss Loves Cafe: for a flavored latte or matcha

At **1655 E. Sixth Street, Suite B-1**, Miss Loves Cafe offers plenty for someone who likes to explore beyond a plain coffee order.

Its merchant-listed DoorDash menu includes Biscoff, banana, and cinnamon Spanish lattes, along with matcha drinks and refreshers. A group with different tastes has several directions to go here. Ask about sweetness and milk options when ordering, rather than assuming every drink comes the same way.

The café also appears on HOPE Collaborative’s 2026 holiday donation drop-off list, a local connection worth watching as the season approaches. [Browse the listed menu](https://www.doordash.com/store/miss-loves-cafe-corona-36784477/).

## Manifested Lab: coffee with a little browsing

Manifested Lab combines a family-owned shop selling fashion, crystals, gemstones, and gifts with its **Secret Cafe**. The business describes both patio seating and an indoor café lounge.

It’s a different sort of coffee outing: you can look around the shop and leave time for a drink. The Dos Lagos business directory includes Manifested Lab, so this stop can fit into a visit to the shopping center.

Check directly for the café’s hours; shopping-center hours don’t necessarily tell you when the coffee service operates. [Visit Manifested Lab](https://manifestedlab.com/).

## Crave Coffee & Tea: coffee and breakfast together

Crave’s **175 E. Ontario Avenue** location gives you options when the coffee run also needs to cover breakfast. Its website lists espresso drinks, cold brew, teas, bagels, and breakfast sandwiches, with availability varying by location.

For a quicker stop, the site provides online pickup ordering. Choose the Ontario Avenue shop carefully: Crave also lists a Corona location on North Maple Street.

Start with the current menu and build the visit around what you need, whether that’s a coffee to carry out or something to eat with it. [Crave’s menu and locations](https://icravecoffee.com/).
