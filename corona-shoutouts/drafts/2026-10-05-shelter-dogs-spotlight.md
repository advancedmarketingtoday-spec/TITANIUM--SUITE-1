# Corona, Your Next Best Friend Could Be Waiting at the Shelter

*An afternoon visit. A first walk together. The beginning of a life with a dog who finally has a place to belong.*

Imagine the ordinary moments a dog could bring to your day: company while you make breakfast, a reason to take that evening walk, or a familiar face waiting when you come home.

At the Corona Animal Shelter, sometimes called the Corona Pound, dogs need people willing to offer that everyday care. For someone ready to adopt, a visit could be the beginning of a lasting friendship.

There is a reason to take the next step now. Sharing an adoptable dog’s listing today can help that dog reach someone who has been thinking about bringing a companion home. You don’t have to rush a lifelong decision. Start by looking, asking questions, and meeting the dogs available for adoption.

## Ready to meet your next companion?

Start with the [City of Corona’s official adoption page](https://www.coronaca.gov/departments/animal-services/adoptable-animals), then choose **Adoptable Dogs**. Individual listings include descriptions and adoption availability dates.

**Corona Animal Shelter**  
1330 Magnolia Avenue  
Corona, CA 92879

- **Posted hours on the adoption page:** Monday–Saturday, **11 a.m.–5:30 p.m. Pacific**.
- **Sundays and holidays:** Listed as closed in the city’s shelter directory.
- **Adoptions and general questions:** [(951) 279-3618](tel:+19512793618).
- **Additional shelter number listed by the city:** [(951) 736-2309](tel:+19517362309).

**Call before visiting.** City pages show conflicting hours. Ask staff to confirm visiting and adoption-processing times, the dog’s availability, current fees, and what to bring.

Tell staff about your household, schedule, and any pets already at home. Ask what they know about the dog’s needs and how to arrange introductions. Finding a good match gives everyone a stronger start.

## A share can help, too

If adopting isn’t possible right now, send the shelter’s adoption page to someone who is ready. Share a verified dog profile with its animal ID and current listing so interested families can find the right animal.

You can also explore the shelter’s [official donation and wish-list page](https://www.coronaca.gov/departments/animal-services/how-you-can-help).

Corona, let’s help these dogs reach the people who can offer them a home, a steady routine, and years of care.
