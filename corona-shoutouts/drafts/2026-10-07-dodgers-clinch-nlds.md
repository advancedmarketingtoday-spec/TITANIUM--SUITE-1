# Dodgers clinch the series, and October gets a little sweeter

*Andy Pages supplied the breakthrough, Max Muncy added breathing room, and a 4-1 win over Atlanta sent Los Angeles to the National League Championship Series.*

For Dodgers fans in Corona, this is a good evening to enjoy the last out a little longer. Los Angeles beat the Atlanta Braves on Wednesday, October 7, at Truist Park, closing the best-of-five National League Division Series three games to one.

There is a particular relief in finishing a playoff series when the chance is there. Fans can put aside the worry of a deciding game and look forward to another round. Tonight, that feels awfully nice.

![Dodger Stadium seating and field at twilight in July 2018](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e4/Dodger_Stadium_twilight.jpg)

## Pages delivers the hit that opens the door

With the score tied in the seventh inning, Andy Pages drove in two runs with a single. Teoscar Hernández had opened the rally with a hit, and walks to Josue De Paula and Kyle Tucker helped load the bases.

Muncy followed with a solo homer in the ninth. Edgardo Henriquez earned the win in relief, Tanner Scott handled the eighth, and Edwin Díaz finished the ninth for the save. [Read the game recap](https://www.truebluela.com/dodgers-scores-standings/126340/andy-pages-single-nlds-braves).

Those late innings give a fan plenty to hold onto: the anticipation before a bases-loaded at-bat, the release when a ball finds space, and the comfort of an insurance run before the final three outs.

## A satisfying finish after Tuesday's groundwork

The Dodgers had moved within one win of advancing with Tuesday's 3-1 victory. Yoshinobu Yamamoto struck out 10 across seven innings, while Kiké Hernández and Freddie Freeman homered. [Tuesday's recap](https://www.reuters.com/sports/baseball/yoshinobu-yamamoto-dodgers-take-nlds-lead-over-braves--flm-2026-10-07/).

Winning the next night spared Los Angeles a Game 5. It also gave fans a clean ending to this chapter of October: two victories in Atlanta and a place in the next round.

That is part of the pleasure of a clincher. You can appreciate the game itself without immediately calculating what has to happen tomorrow to keep the season alive.

## A happy baseball conversation for Corona

If you follow the Dodgers from Corona, you do not need to be in the ballpark to enjoy this one. The next conversation with a fellow fan already has a starting point: Where were you when Pages came through?

Maybe you watched every pitch. Maybe you followed the score between dinner and the rest of your evening. Either way, a series win is a lovely excuse to check in with someone who shares your team.

For families watching together, it is also a chance to talk about the smaller plays that keep an inning moving. A walk can matter. A relief appearance can matter. There is room in a playoff win for several players to give fans something to remember.

## Another round begins Sunday

The Dodgers are headed to their third straight NLCS. The series is scheduled to begin Sunday, October 11, against either Milwaukee or San Diego.
