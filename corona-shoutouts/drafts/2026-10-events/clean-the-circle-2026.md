# Clean the Circle 2026: Volunteer in Corona on October 17

Corona residents looking for a hands-on way to help their community can join the City of Corona's **Clean the Circle** volunteer event on Saturday, October 17, 2026.

The cleanup is scheduled for **8:00 AM to 10:00 AM**, with volunteer check-in beginning at **7:30 AM**. The City lists the location as **The Circle, Corona**.

## What volunteers should know

The City describes Clean the Circle as an all-ages community cleanup. Volunteers should wear closed-toe comfortable shoes and clothes they do not mind getting dirty; jeans are recommended. Work gloves and safety vests will be provided, along with water.

Teens must be at least 14 years old to volunteer without a parent present. Participants under 18 need a parent to sign their liability waiver.

The City says volunteer parking information will be shared during the week of the event. Registration is listed through **October 14 at 5:00 PM**. The City also notes that it cannot validate court- or school-mandated disciplinary service hours.

Questions about the event can be directed to the City's listed number, **951-736-2241**.

## Why this event matters locally

Community cleanups give residents a direct way to care for one of Corona's most recognizable areas while meeting neighbors and other volunteers. For Corona Shoutouts, it is also the kind of positive local participation we want to highlight: residents giving their time to improve the community.
