# Clean the Circle in Corona: October 17 Volunteer Guide

*A two-hour cleanup gives neighbors a hands-on way to care for Corona’s historic Circle.*

Corona’s **Clean the Circle** community cleanup is scheduled for Saturday, October 17, 2026. Volunteer check-in begins at **7:30 a.m.**, and the cleanup runs **8–10 a.m.**

The current City event listing identifies the location as **The Circle, Corona** and tells volunteers to look for the City booth and flags at check-in. Registration closes **Wednesday, October 14, at 5 p.m.**

That deadline matters if you are organizing a group or hoping to bring family members. Use the [official event listing](https://community.coronaca.gov/coronaca_main/260112147) and its registration control rather than treating this article as a sign-up form.

### Register first, then watch for instructions

The City says volunteers will receive an email confirmation on October 14 and that parking details will be shared during the week of the event. Keep an eye on the email account used to register, including its spam folder.

A partner calendar lists a civic address, but the current City listing does not confirm that address as the volunteer meeting point. This draft therefore does not direct readers to City Hall as the cleanup check-in site. Follow the City’s registration email for the final arrival and parking instructions.

If those instructions do not arrive, the event listing gives **951-736-2241** for questions. Confirm your arrangements before heading out rather than choosing a meeting point from an older announcement.

### What volunteers need to know

The event welcomes all ages. Teenagers **14 and older** may volunteer without a parent, while volunteers **under 18** need a parent waiver. These are separate rules: permission to attend independently does not remove the waiver requirement.

Wear comfortable closed-toe shoes and clothes that can get dirty. The City recommends jeans and says gloves, safety vests and water will be provided. Check the forecast and choose clothing suitable for an outdoor morning; the listing does not describe a rain contingency.

The City also says it cannot validate court-ordered or school-mandated disciplinary service hours. Anyone who needs signed service documentation should clarify eligibility before registering. Do not assume every kind of required service can be fulfilled here.

### Connect the cleanup with Corona’s history

The Circle is more than a route around town. Our [Grand Boulevard history article](https://coronashoutouts.com/a/corona-history-grand-boulevard-road-races) provides context for a place volunteers will be helping care for.

You can find other local opportunities through the [Corona Shoutouts event calendar](https://www.coronashoutouts.com/events). A cleanup can also be a manageable first community activity for a new resident: there is a defined work window and clear clothing guidance.

For this event, the next steps are to register by **October 14 at 5 p.m.**, read the confirmation email, and arrive for **7:30 a.m. check-in** at the location the City confirms. Bring the required waiver if applicable and be ready for the 8 a.m. work start.
