# EMPOWERED Women’s Safety and Wellness Event in Corona: October 10

*A free Saturday morning brings safety education, wellness activities and community resources together.*

Corona Police Department is hosting **EMPOWERED: Women’s Safety and Wellness Event** on Saturday, October 10, 2026, from **9 a.m. to noon**. The City of Corona’s organizer listing describes it as the first annual event and says admission is free.

The event takes place at the **Corona Police Department, 730 Public Safety Way, Corona, CA 92880**. Its announced focus is safety, wellness, confidence and connection for women in the community.

The program description includes self-defense and safety education, wellness activities, local vendors, community resources, giveaways and opportunities to connect with police department staff. It does not provide an instructor list or a session-by-session timetable.

### A practical introduction to local resources

For a resident who has wanted to learn about local safety resources but has not known where to start, a community event can make that first conversation easier. The organizer frames this morning around learning and connection.

Come with the questions that matter to you. If a particular activity, instructor credential or accommodation will determine whether you attend, ask the organizer beforehand rather than assuming it is included in the broad program description.

This preview is an event announcement, not medical guidance or a promise that one session will prepare anyone for every unsafe situation. We have not assessed the self-defense curriculum or the wellness activities, and the organizer has not supplied those details in the listing reviewed.

### Check the organizer’s page before arriving

Use the [City of Corona’s Eventbrite listing](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/empowered-womens-safety-and-wellness-event-tickets-2000496153848) for the current event information and its ticket/contact controls. The retrieved page shows a ticket button, but we did not complete a booking or establish whether registration is mandatory.

The [official police department page](https://www.coronaca.gov/departments/police) independently confirms the venue address. Event questions belong with the organizer; emergency channels should remain available for emergencies.

Parking instructions, minimum-age rules, accessibility details and a complete vendor roster were not verified. Avoid planning around an unannounced workshop or giveaway. Schedule changes are also possible, so check again near the event date.

### Share useful details with a friend

If you invite someone, send the official link along with the **9 a.m.–noon** time window and Public Safety Way address. That makes it easier for them to check the program and ask questions independently.

For more ways to connect locally, browse the [Corona Shoutouts event calendar](https://www.coronashoutouts.com/events) and our [local article collection](https://coronashoutouts.com/articles).

The confirmed basics are enough to make a first plan: a free community morning, a police department venue and an announced mix of safety education and wellness resources. Check the organizer’s current instructions before attending and choose the activities that fit your interests.
