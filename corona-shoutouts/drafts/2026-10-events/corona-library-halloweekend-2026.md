# Corona Library Halloweekend 2026: Story Time, STEM Fun and Treats

Kids and their families can kick off Corona's two-day Halloweekend celebration at the **Corona Public Library** on **Friday, October 9, 2026**.

The library's spooktacular day features hands-on STEM activities, a Halloween-themed story time, a friendly round of BINGO, and sweet treats for kids and parents alike. Little ones — and big ones — are invited to come dressed as their favorite hero or villain and join in the fun.

## What's happening at the library

- **Hands-on STEM activities** — playful, hands-on science and building fun for kids
- **Halloween-themed story time** — a cozy read-aloud with a spooky (but friendly) twist
- **BINGO** — a lively game everyone can play
- **Treats** — sweet goodies for all ages
- **Costumes welcome** — come as your favorite hero, villain, or storybook character

The City describes the library day as an all-ages celebration of learning, laughter, and Halloween excitement.

## Part of a bigger Halloweekend

The library event is the opening day of Corona's **Halloweekend**: A Tale of Heroes and Villains. The celebration continues on **Saturday, October 10** with the community **Treat Trail** from 5–7 p.m. on the HCC Front Lawn (815 W. 6th St.), followed by a family movie on the lawn starting at 7 p.m. — bring blankets and chairs.

## Why this matters to Corona families

Library events like this give neighbors an easy, free way to celebrate the season together — combining reading, learning, and play in a safe, welcoming space. For Corona Shoutouts, it's the kind of positive, family-friendly community connection we love to share.

## Before you go

The library is located at **650 S. Main St., Corona, CA 92882**. Exact program hours for the library day were not listed on the city announcement; confirm times and any details with the Corona Public Library or its event calendar before heading out.
