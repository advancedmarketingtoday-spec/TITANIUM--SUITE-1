# Halloweekend at Corona Public Library: Heroes and Villains on October 9

*Costumes, stories and STEM activities open Corona’s two-day Halloween celebration.*

The name to put on your calendar is **Halloweekend**. Corona Public Library’s “Tale of Heroes and Villains” celebration begins Friday, October 9, 2026, at **5:30 p.m.** The City lists it as a free event for all ages.

The library is at **650 South Main Street, Corona, CA 92882**. Friday’s program includes STEM activities, Halloween Story Time, BINGO and treats. Attendees are invited to dress as a favorite hero or villain.

That mix gives families several ways to join in, whether a child is excited about a costume or more interested in an activity. The organizer’s description does not give separate activity times, so plan around the announced opening rather than assuming there is a fixed rotation.

### Friday is at the library

This preview covers the Friday library event. The official listing reviewed provides a beginning time but no confirmed finish time. It also does not establish that reservations are required—or guarantee entry without any capacity limits.

Check the [City’s library event listing](https://community.coronaca.gov/coronaca_main/260202779) before visiting. For questions about accessibility, costume guidelines or participation needs, use the [Corona Public Library information page](https://www.coronaca.gov/departments/library), which lists the library phone as **951-736-2381**.

The general library operating schedule should not be substituted for the event schedule. Likewise, a mention of treats does not identify ingredients or promise an allergy-safe option. Families who need specific accommodations can ask the library in advance.

### Saturday is a separate stop

The [City’s Halloweekend announcement](https://www.coronaca.gov/news/halloweekend) describes a second celebration on Saturday, October 10. Its Treat Trail runs **5–7 p.m.** on the **Historic Civic Center front lawn, 815 W. 6th St.**, followed by a movie at **7 p.m.**

Saturday’s announcement mentions games, music, a maze, photo opportunities and giveaways. It suggests blankets and chairs for the movie. The material reviewed does not name the film, so this draft does not supply one.

These are different venues and different starting times. If you are coordinating with friends, say “Friday at the library” or “Saturday at the Civic Center” rather than sharing only the Halloweekend name.

### Build a simple weekend plan

Browse our [Corona weekend guides](https://coronashoutouts.com/articles/corona-weekend-guides) for other local ideas, and use the [community event calendar](https://www.coronashoutouts.com/events) to see what else is scheduled.

For the library celebration, the practical next step is straightforward: check the official page, choose your costume if you want one, and head to South Main Street for the 5:30 p.m. start. Keep Saturday’s Treat Trail in a separate calendar entry so the two events stay easy to follow.
