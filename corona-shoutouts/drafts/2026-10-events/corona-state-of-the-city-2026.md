# Corona’s 2026 State of the City: October 8 at the Historic Civic Center

*A free evening offers residents a chance to hear about Corona’s progress and what comes next.*

Corona’s 2026 State of the City is scheduled for Thursday, October 8, at the Historic Civic Center Theater. The City’s announcement calls this year’s theme **Grand by Design**, with an address focused on progress, projects and the community’s future.

For residents who want to follow local decisions beyond a headline, this is an opportunity to hear the City’s presentation directly. It can also be a useful introduction for someone who has recently moved here and wants to understand the priorities being discussed.

### The evening’s schedule

The official listing gives three times: pre-show entertainment begins at **6 p.m.**, the State of the City address starts at **6:30 p.m.**, and a social reception begins at **7 p.m.** The reception time is a start time; the organizer has not supplied an event end time in the listing reviewed for this draft.

The theater is at **815 W. 6th St., Corona, CA 92882**. Use that venue address when planning your trip rather than assuming the event is at City Hall.

Admission is free, but space is limited. The City asks attendees to RSVP to reserve a seat and links to [its Eventbrite registration page](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/2000193497595?aff=oddtdtcreator). We verified that registration link appears on the City’s event listing; we could not independently confirm remaining ticket availability.

### What to expect—and what to check

The published announcement describes the broad theme, not a detailed list of promises or a transcript of the address. This preview therefore does not predict project announcements, identify an unconfirmed speaker or present future remarks as something already said.

Before leaving, revisit the [official event listing](https://community.coronaca.gov/coronaca_main/260156257) for any schedule or access updates. The material reviewed does not establish a parking arrangement, livestream, accessibility accommodation process or reserved-seat policy beyond the RSVP instruction. Ask the organizer about any accommodation you need before attending.

If you are bringing someone along, share the registration link as well as the address. An RSVP is more useful than a calendar reminder alone when the organizer warns that seating is limited.

### Make it part of your local reading

The event takes place in a part of Corona with a long civic history. Our [Grand Boulevard history article](https://coronashoutouts.com/a/corona-history-grand-boulevard-road-races) offers background for readers who want to learn more about the Circle and the city around it.

You can also browse the [Corona Shoutouts event calendar](https://www.coronashoutouts.com/events) for other community plans. For this evening, start with the official RSVP, leave time for arrival before the address, and keep the City’s page handy for last-minute changes.
