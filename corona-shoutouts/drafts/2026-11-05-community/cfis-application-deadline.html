Talk about the school day your child needs

An application deadline is also a chance for a calm family conversation. Ask your child what helps them learn, where they feel confident, and what they hope will be different or familiar in intermediate school. Let them ask questions that matter to them, even when those questions are about ordinary daily routines.

Then consider the practical side together. What would a school morning look like? Who would handle transportation? How would the household manage after-school responsibilities? Confirm the school's actual schedules and available arrangements rather than assuming they match another campus your family knows.

Families can also ask whom to contact about learning supports, accessibility, or other needs important to their child. You do not need to settle every future detail before asking for information. A clear question now can help you understand the next step and make a more informed choice.
