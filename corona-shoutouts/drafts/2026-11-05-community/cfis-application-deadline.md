# Corona Families: CFIS Applications Are Due November 20 at 3 P.M.

![Illustration of a parent and child reviewing a school application at home](images/cfis-hero.jpg)

*AI-generated illustration; not an actual CFIS family, application screen, or school facility.*

Families considering Corona Fundamental Intermediate School for an incoming seventh grader in 2027–28 have an important deadline ahead: the online application is due Friday, November 20, 2026, at 3 p.m. Start at the school's official Lottery & Registration Information page, where CFIS links the application and its current admissions information.

This is a friendly follow-up to our live school-choice guide, with one practical goal: help interested families finish the right application on time. There is still room to review the information, talk with your child, and ask questions before a busy Friday afternoon arrives.

## Put the exact deadline on the family calendar

The [CFIS admissions page](https://cfis.cnusd.k12.ca.us/our_school/lottery_and_registration_information) confirms that applications are open for incoming seventh-grade students for the 2027–2028 academic year. The [district announcement](https://www.cnusd.k12.ca.us/c_n_u_s_d_c_o_n_n_e_c_t_i_o_n/c_f_i_s_information_night_and_registration) says the window opened October 26 and closes November 20 at 3 p.m.

This application concerns next school year's incoming seventh graders. The closing time is in the afternoon, so do not plan around an assumed midnight deadline. Add the school year and time to your reminder, particularly if your household is handling applications for children at more than one grade level.

A helpful family target is to finish before deadline day. That gives you time to check entries, clarify an instruction, or deal with an interruption. Submitting early is a planning suggestion; it is not a claim that an earlier application improves admission chances or creates priority.

## Use the school's current application link

Open the official admissions page and choose its online-application link. Going through the school website helps you avoid using an old form saved in a family chat or a bookmark from another year. Check the heading and school year before entering student information.

Read the full instructions first. Then gather only the information the current form requests, using a reliable contact email that your household checks. We have not reproduced a document checklist here because families should follow the school's actual application rather than a guessed list of requirements.

Before submitting, review the spelling of names, your contact details, and every required response. If you are unsure how to answer a question, contact CFIS for clarification. Guessing now can create a second task later, while a short question may settle the issue before you finish.

After submitting, retain whatever confirmation the form provides. Save a screenshot or receipt if available, and keep a note of when you completed it. If the form offers no clear confirmation or you cannot tell whether submission worked, ask the school how to verify receipt.

## Missed information night? Check the recordings

CNUSD's announcement listed an in-person information session for October 27, which has already passed. It also said recorded presentations in English and Spanish would be available beginning October 30. The current CFIS admissions page links an English presentation and labels the Spanish presentation as coming soon.

That difference is useful for families to know. If you need the Spanish presentation or help understanding an instruction, contact the school for the latest availability and assistance. The earlier announcement should not be taken as proof that both recordings are now accessible.

When you review a presentation, jot down questions as they arise. You might want clarification about the application process, the school's expectations, communication with families, or what happens after the window closes. Keep those questions together so a conversation with the office can be focused and useful.

## Talk about the school day your child needs

An application deadline is also a chance for a calm family conversation. Ask your child what helps them learn, where they feel confident, and what they hope will be different or familiar in intermediate school. Let them ask questions that matter to them, even when those questions are about ordinary daily routines.

Then consider the practical side together. What would a school morning look like? Who would handle transportation? How would the household manage after-school responsibilities? Confirm the school's actual schedules and available arrangements rather than assuming they match another campus your family knows.

Families can also ask whom to contact about learning supports, accessibility, or other needs important to their child. You do not need to settle every future detail before asking for information. A clear question now can help you understand the next step and make a more informed choice.

## Keep each school-choice process separate

Our [CNUSD school-choice article](https://coronashoutouts.com/a/cnusd-school-choice-cfis-dual-language-mandarin-immersion-2027-28) places CFIS alongside the district's Spanish dual-language and Mandarin immersion options. It is a useful starting point for households comparing opportunities for children of different ages.

For this reminder, however, the November 20 deadline applies specifically to the CFIS incoming seventh-grade application described by the school. Do not transfer that date, grade level, or procedure to a different program. Follow each program's official instructions and keep separate calendar entries when necessary.

The CFIS website calls this its Lottery & Registration Information section. An application submission should not be treated as an admission offer or completed enrollment. Ask the school about selection procedures, notifications, and any later steps; no result date, acceptance guarantee, or waitlist position is being announced here.

If two caregivers are handling the process, agree on who will submit the application and who will check it afterward. One shared plan can prevent uncertainty about whether someone else has already finished.

## A friendly final check for Corona parents

Before you put the task away, make sure the application is submitted, your confirmation is saved if one was provided, and the adults sharing responsibility know it is done. Continue checking the email address you supplied and follow any instructions the school sends afterward.

CFIS lists its campus at 1230 South Main Street, Corona, and its office phone as 951-736-3321. Use that official contact for application questions. Keep student records and private application information with the school rather than posting them in public comments or community message threads.

Wishing every family a thoughtful, manageable school-choice season.
