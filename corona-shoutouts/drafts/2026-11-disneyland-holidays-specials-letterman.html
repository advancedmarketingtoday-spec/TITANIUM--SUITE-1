Disneyland in November 2026: Holiday Magic, Christmas Celebrations, Special Offers & Family Fun

November 2026 brings Christmas parades, glowing castles, spectacular nighttime entertainment, festive dining, and seasonal savings to Disneyland Resort in Anaheim. Disney’s holiday season officially begins Friday, November 13, 2026, and runs through January 6, 2027. For Corona and Inland Empire families considering a special outing, here is what’s confirmed, what costs extra, and how to plan a memorable visit.

November 13: The Holidays Officially Begin

The first part of November provides a transition from the fall season to Christmas festivities. Disney lists its Plaza de la Familia celebration through November 2 and Veterans Day on November 11. The main holiday program begins November 13 at both Disneyland Park and Disney California Adventure. Holiday decorations, music, character greetings, food and entertainment are included in the seasonal lineup, though park admission and a park reservation are still required for park entry.

For visitors arriving from Corona, the choice is more than which attraction to ride first. November is a chance to experience favorite childhood traditions with parents, grandparents, and youngsters, all within a Southern California day trip.

A Christmas Fantasy Parade Returns

At Disneyland Park, A Christmas Fantasy Parade once again fills the route with marching toy soldiers, dancing gingerbread cookies, lively reindeer, princesses, beloved characters and Santa Claus. Disney says the procession lasts about 40 minutes. Main Street, U.S.A. offers a classic viewpoint, but the route near “it’s a small world” and the Matterhorn can also be appealing.

Check the Disneyland app for your specific visit date: parades may not run every day, and schedules can change. Arriving early for a viewing spot can be especially helpful for families with young children.

Fireworks, Snowfall and a Winter Castle

When the sun sets, Sleeping Beauty Castle becomes Sleeping Beauty’s Winter Castle, decorated with wreaths, shimmering icicles and more than 80,000 lights. The Wintertime Enchantment moment adds a sparkling glow and a gentle snowfall effect at selected park locations.

The returning “Believe…in Holiday Magic” nighttime spectacular combines seasonal music with fireworks and simulated snowfall, weather permitting. Even for guests who have visited Disneyland many times, an illuminated castle offers a beautiful setting for a family picture. Entertainment is subject to operational and weather changes.

Holiday Makeovers for Favorite Attractions

Two beloved Disneyland attractions receive seasonal transformations. Haunted Mansion Holiday, inspired by Tim Burton’s The Nightmare Before Christmas, features festive storytelling and a special gingerbread display. Disney says 2026 marks the 25th year of its elaborate holiday gingerbread creations, with a music-box-inspired ballroom display.

Meanwhile, “it’s a small world” Holiday fills the attraction with seasonal music and decorations. Outside, hundreds of thousands of lights illuminate its distinctive façade. Both offer an especially festive way to enjoy attractions that generations of California families already know and love.

Disney California Adventure: Festival of Holidays

Across the esplanade, Disney Festival of Holidays celebrates different cultural traditions through music, dance and seasonal flavors from November 13 through January 6. Six Festive Food Marketplaces are scheduled to serve new dishes alongside returning favorites, including braised pork belly adobo.

For guests who enjoy sampling several foods, the separately purchased Sip and Savor Pass provides six digital coupons redeemable for eligible food and nonalcoholic beverages at participating locations. Check the menu and prices before buying; specialty food and drinks are not automatically included with park admission.

Live entertainment brings additional warmth. Look for the Holiday Toy Drummers, Mickey’s Happy Holidays cavalcade, and select-day performances such as Mirabel’s Gifts of the Season. Disney ¡Viva Navidad! celebrates Latin American traditions through colorful performances, while Mariachi Alegría de Disneyland and Miguel from Coco share bilingual holiday music on selected dates.

World of Color and a Festive Cars Land

Disney California Adventure brings back “World of Color – Season of Light”, a nighttime spectacular featuring holiday music and scenes from Disney and Pixar favorites, including Beauty and the Beast and Inside Out.

Cars Land also joins the celebration. Holiday ornaments dress up Radiator Springs, while Mater’s Jingle Jamboree and Luigi’s Joy to the Whirl offer seasonal twists on familiar rides. Families who love colorful decorations may especially enjoy Cars Land just after sunset.

Meet Santa and Enjoy Seasonal Treats

From November 13 through December 24, Santa visits a new Disneyland location at Mickey’s Toontown Trolley Barn, near Roger Rabbit’s Car Toon Spin. Guests can also find him at Redwood Creek Challenge Trail in Disney California Adventure. Select appearances are planned at the three Disneyland Resort hotels.

Holiday food is part of the fun. Disney has announced a mint-and-chocolate-cookie Mickey elf shake at Schmoozies! and a merry cranberry crispy chicken sandwich at Galactic Grill. Collectible seasonal novelties include the Mickey Train Light-Up popcorn bucket and a Holiday Stitch Christmas Tree sipper. Availability and prices may vary, and popular souvenirs may sell out.

Downtown Disney Offers More Festive Fun

Families do not have to spend the entire day inside a theme park to enjoy seasonal atmosphere. Beginning November 13, Downtown Disney District features holiday décor, seasonal menus, entertainment and nightly simulated snowfall. Its LIVE! Stage will host performers, DJs and bands.

Chip and Dale’s Ornament Trail starts November 11 and runs through January 6. Guests purchase a map, search for ornaments around the shopping district, and redeem their completed activity for a holiday keepsake. Participation in the activity costs extra.

November Specials: Hotel Savings and Seasonal Dining

Disney is advertising up to 20% off select Disneyland Resort hotel stays of three nights or longer, with qualifying travel from October 11 through December 18, 2026. The offer must be booked by December 15, subject to availability, hotel selection and restrictions. It is a lodging promotion, not a blanket discount on theme-park tickets.

Three holiday character-dining experiences are also returning: Goofy’s Kitchen Celebrates the Holidays! at Disneyland Hotel, plus Daisy’s Holiday Pajama Party breakfast and brunch and Mickey’s Christmas Carol Feast dinner at Storytellers Cafe. Disney’s Grand Californian Hotel & Spa is also bringing back its seven-foot gingerbread display. Dining costs extra and reservations should be checked directly with Disney.

Another optional experience is the Holiday Time at the Disneyland Resort Guided Tour, featuring seasonal stories and reserved viewing for A Christmas Fantasy Parade, subject to the tour’s terms and additional price.

Tips for Corona Families Planning a November Visit

Before leaving the Inland Empire, choose your park date, review admission and reservation requirements, and check the official entertainment calendar. A one-day visit can focus on one park, while a multi-day trip creates more room for parades, shows, rides and a relaxed meal. Keep younger guests’ energy in mind, pack an evening layer, and allow flexibility for changing showtimes.

For families prioritizing holiday entertainment, November 13 or later is the key period to consider. If you mainly want decorations, a meal and shopping, Downtown Disney may offer a simpler outing. Either way, November brings plenty of opportunities to make warm memories together.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do the Christmas celebrations start at Disneyland in 2026?

Holidays at Disneyland Resort begin November 13, 2026, and continue through January 6, 2027.

Does a Disneyland ticket include Festival of Holidays?

The festival’s entertainment is included with valid Disney California Adventure admission, but food, beverages and the optional Sip and Savor Pass cost extra. A reservation is also required for park entry.

Are there Disneyland discounts for November 2026?

Disney is offering up to 20% off select hotel stays of three or more nights during a qualifying autumn and holiday travel window. Eligibility and availability vary; review the current official offer before booking.
