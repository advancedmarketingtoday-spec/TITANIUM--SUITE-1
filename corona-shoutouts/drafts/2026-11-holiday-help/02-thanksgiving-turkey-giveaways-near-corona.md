# Thanksgiving Turkey Giveaways Near Corona: Verified November 2026 Dates and Planning Tips

**Corona families looking for a Thanksgiving turkey have several confirmed regional opportunities in November 2026.** A free drive-through giveaway is scheduled in **Ontario on Friday, November 13**, a separate drive-through takes place in **Covina on Sunday, November 22**, and a Thanksgiving community event offering meals and turkey/grocery assistance is announced for **Fontana on Wednesday, November 25**. Check each organizer's rules before driving.

![Thanksgiving turkey giveaways near Corona](./images/corona_thanksgiving_turkey_giveaways.png)

A turkey may be the centerpiece of a holiday dinner, but planning one can be difficult when grocery costs rise and several relatives or neighbors are sharing the table. Community giveaways are a reminder of how many organizations and volunteers work together to help. They are also time-sensitive: an event can end early when supplies are gone, and a social post from a different year can send families to the wrong place.

For this guide, Corona Shoutouts is separating confirmed 2026 events from programs that have not announced precise dates. The locations below are **regional, not in Corona city limits**. Families should consider transportation, travel time and whether an event is intended for residents of a particular service area.

## Friday, November 13: Free turkey drive-through in Ontario

The published announcement for Assemblymember Michelle Rodriguez's **Second Annual Drive-Thru Turkey Giveaway** lists **Friday, November 13, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m.** at the **ONT Field parking lot, 9375 E. Riverside Drive, Ontario, CA 91761**. The notice says **free frozen turkeys are available while supplies last, with no RSVP needed**.

This is a drive-through event, so confirm whether your vehicle and arrival arrangements meet the organizer's guidelines. No RSVP does not mean a turkey is reserved for every person who arrives. If your household needs other groceries as well, ask whether any food items beyond the turkey are offered; the announcement itself prominently confirms the turkey.

If you have a freezer at home, allow enough time to bring the turkey back and store it safely. A frozen bird is not a ready-to-eat meal. Households without an oven or cooking equipment may be better served by a community meal or a prepared-food program.

## Sunday, November 22: Operation Gobble in Covina

A second verified option is **Operation Gobble**, organized through Assemblywoman Blanca E. Rubio's office with community partners. It is scheduled for **Sunday, November 22, 2026, from 9 a.m. to noon**, at **Charter Oak High School, 1430 E. Covina Boulevard, Covina, CA 91724**.

The organizer listing describes a **free frozen turkey or turkey voucher plus food items**, limited to **one per household while supplies last**. Importantly, the event is **drive-through only; no walk-ups**. Those are meaningful distinctions for anyone depending on another person's ride or using public transportation. Organizers also identify an Assembly District 48 service area, so Corona residents should contact the office before assuming eligibility.

The listed information line is **(626) 960-4457**. Ask whether Corona residents may participate, whether proof of address is needed, and if vouchers have restrictions or expiration dates. An accurate phone call is better than a wasted round trip.

## Wednesday, November 25: Eat & Be Well in Fontana

Project Boon's **Eat & Be Well Thanksgiving** event is scheduled for **Wednesday, November 25, 2026**, at the **Cypress Neighborhood Center, 8380 Cypress Avenue, Fontana, CA 92335**. The organizer's event description says it plans to serve hot Thanksgiving meals beginning at **11 a.m.** and distribute whole uncooked turkeys and holiday groceries to eligible or under-resourced families.

The organizers have discussed serving more than a thousand hot meals and helping hundreds of households with groceries. These are plans, not guarantees of inventory for an individual visitor. A volunteer sign-up page also shows an all-day window; do not confuse volunteer shift hours with meal distribution times. For attendees, use the organizer's **11 a.m.** description, and verify details close to the date.

Fontana may be a useful option for households that want a prepared meal as well as those seeking ingredients for dinner. Confirm whether turkey/grocery packages require registration or a separate line, and whether they are available to families traveling from Corona.

## What about the WOW Flower Project?

An earlier planning note mentioned a WOW Flower Project meal giveaway and a **November 13 application deadline**. We could not independently confirm a current official 2026 application notice with those details. That means this article is **not presenting it as a verified opportunity**. If a reader has the organization's current flyer, they should follow its direct contact information and verify the offer, deadline, address and eligibility before applying.

The same principle applies to older social-media listings for holiday events. A picture of a turkey and a familiar logo do not establish that an event is happening this year. Dates, locations and organizers should all match.

## How to prepare for a giveaway day

Plan for traffic and lines, but do not assume that arriving extremely early guarantees service. Bring water, necessary medications and a charged phone if the event might involve waiting. Keep a cooler or a safe transport plan available for frozen food, particularly on a warm Southern California afternoon. Follow volunteers' instructions on site so distribution can move smoothly.

Make one short checklist before leaving: exact address, event time, vehicle versus walk-up rules, registration requirements, proof of residence, household limit and the organizer's current announcement. If the giveaway is a significant distance from Corona, ask about service-area restrictions before committing to the drive.

For anyone helping a relative or senior neighbor, make sure the organizer allows proxy pickup. “One per household” rules may prevent a driver from collecting multiple packages even when their intentions are generous. Never promise someone a turkey until an organizer confirms availability.

## If you cannot travel outside Corona

The **Corona-Norco Settlement House**, at **507 S. Vicentia Avenue**, maintains a regular food pantry for qualifying local residents. The published pantry hours are **weekdays, 4–5:30 p.m.**, and its phone number is **(951) 737-3504**. This is **not** a claim that a special turkey giveaway is happening there on a particular November date.

Other local food pantries are listed by Corona-Norco United Way, and calling **2-1-1** can help identify additional resources. Explain whether your household needs food to cook, a complete holiday grocery package or a hot meal. Each option solves a different problem.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are the turkey giveaways free?** The published Ontario and Covina events describe free turkey distributions. Fontana's organizer announces a community Thanksgiving event with hot meals and turkey/grocery assistance.

**Can I walk up?** Covina explicitly says no. Ontario is advertised as drive-through. Confirm the Fontana logistics directly with Project Boon.

**Is Nov. 13 the same event as Nov. 22?** No. The November 13 event is in Ontario and the November 22 event is in Covina.

**Will supplies last all day?** None of the event notices guarantees that. Ask the organizer for updates rather than treating the closing time as a promise.
