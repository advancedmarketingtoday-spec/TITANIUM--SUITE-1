Corona, CA Spotlight: Neighbors Helping Neighbors at Settlement House

A local tradition of care, with everyday ways to help and an October 17 fundraiser

Corona-Norco Settlement House gives residents practical ways to care for neighbors through volunteering, donations and its community thrift store. Based at 507 S. Vicentia Avenue in Corona, the nonprofit is also inviting supporters to its October 17, 2026, Empty Bowl fundraiser in Norco. Here is how to take part and connect with its work.

From Corona’s Citrus Groves to Today’s Community Support

According to the organization’s history page, the Woman’s Improvement Club founded its predecessor, Associated Charities, in 1912 to assist workers arriving for Corona’s citrus harvests. The agency became Settlement House in 1918. That origin connects its work to the people who helped build the city’s agricultural economy.

Today, its stated service area follows Corona-Norco Unified School District boundaries. The City of Corona’s nonprofit directory also lists the organization and its Vicentia Avenue address. Residents can contact the office to learn about current services and eligibility.

The programs page lists a weekday food pantry and thrift store. The pantry offers bagged groceries to qualifying households with cooking facilities, with eligibility screening and visits once every 30 days. The thrift store welcomes the wider community, and the organization says purchases support its mission.

  • Location: 507 S. Vicentia Avenue, Corona, CA 92882.
  • Food pantry: Monday–Friday, 4–5:30 p.m. Pacific; eligibility screening applies.
  • Thrift store: Monday–Friday, 10 a.m.–5 p.m. Pacific.
  • Office: (951) 737-3504; call to confirm services before visiting.

Find a Volunteer Role That Fits Your Time

The organization’s volunteer page offers several ways to help. Thrift-store volunteers assist shoppers and restock shelves, while donation sorters prepare items for distribution or resale. Office support, grounds care and event assistance are also listed.

These roles give residents options based on their interests and schedules. Someone comfortable greeting visitors can ask about store or office work; someone who enjoys organizing can ask about sorting donations. This is a practical way to choose a starting point, with assignments arranged by the organization.

The page welcomes volunteers of all ages and says those under 16 must be accompanied by an adult. Call the office or use the volunteer contact listed on the page to ask about openings, orientation and suitable tasks. Confirm donation needs before gathering items so your contribution matches what staff can use.

Support the Empty Bowl Fundraiser on October 17

The organization’s current homepage announces its 17th Empty Bowl fundraiser for Saturday, October 17, 2026. The gathering takes place in Norco, while supporting the nonprofit based in Corona. Proceeds go to Settlement House.

  • When: Saturday, October 17, 2026, noon–4 p.m. Pacific.
  • Where: Turning Point Church, 2000 Norco Drive, Norco.
  • Tickets: $35 for adults; $20 for children under 10.
  • Included: Soup, salad, dessert, a beverage and a bowl.
  • Activities: Entertainment, raffles and silent auctions.
  • Purchase: The homepage lists online PayPal purchases and tickets at the door. Confirm pricing for ages 10–17 and any availability questions with the organizer.

Use the current homepage announcement when planning, and call the office to confirm details before buying tickets or traveling. The separate event page still displays an older year. The homepage’s October 17, 2026, announcement is the source used here.
