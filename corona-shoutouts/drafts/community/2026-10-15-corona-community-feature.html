Dos Lagos & Corona Weekend Planner: October 16–18, 2026

A Saturday volunteer morning, lakeside time and easy outings close to home

Corona’s October 16–18 weekend offers a chance to lend a hand at Clean the Circle, browse books at Corona Public Library or spend time outdoors at Dos Lagos and Eagle Glen Park. Check volunteer registration today, then choose the outing that fits your household. All dates and times below are Pacific.

Help care for the Circle on Saturday morning

The city’s Clean the Circle listing invites neighbors to volunteer around Corona’s Grand Boulevard Circle on Saturday, October 17. Registration is listed through Thursday, October 15, at 4 p.m. Check the linked registration portal for availability before making plans.

  • Check-in begins at 7:30 a.m.; the cleanup runs from 8 to 10 a.m.
  • The listed location is the Circle in Corona. Use the city’s participant instructions for the exact check-in and parking location.
  • All ages are welcome. Volunteers younger than 14 need a parent present, and everyone under 18 needs a parent-signed waiver.
  • Wear closed-toed shoes and comfortable clothing that can get dirty. The city recommends jeans and supplies gloves, safety vests and water.
  • The listing does not show a participation fee. Questions: (951) 736-2241.

For a household that enjoys working together, this offers a defined morning activity with a clear finish time. Arrange participation with the city first, especially when bringing younger volunteers.

Make Dos Lagos a lakeside stop, with no concert assumption

The Shops at Dos Lagos has two lakes, a pedestrian bridge and places to shop or eat. A walk paired with a meal is a flexible option for this weekend. These are regular amenities, not a scheduled festival or guided outing.

The venue’s current event calendar lists its next event on October 24. We could not verify an Amphitheater performance for October 16–18, so plan this visit around the lakes and businesses. Check individual business hours before choosing a dining stop.

  • Shopping center address: 2780 Cabot Drive, Corona, CA 92883.
  • Posted center hours: Friday and Saturday, 10 a.m. to 9 p.m.; Sunday, 11 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • No event ticket is identified for the suggested self-guided visit. Meals, shopping and any separately ticketed activities cost extra.

The center’s visitor information describes the lakeside setting and bridge. For questions about access or hours, contact mall management at (951) 277-7601.

Choose books or a park visit for a quieter outing

Corona Public Library: Friday or Saturday browsing

The library at 650 South Main Street posts Friday hours of 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. and Saturday hours of 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.; it is closed Sunday. Bring the family to browse and choose books during those regular hours. Ask staff about borrowing requirements if you need a library card.

No specific October 16–18 program could be verified from the library calendar during this check. Treat this as a browsing suggestion, not an advertised storytime or workshop. For current programs and visit details, call (951) 736-2381.

Eagle Glen Park: playground and picnic space

Eagle Glen Park, at 4190 Bennett Avenue, has a playground, picnic areas, restrooms and sports facilities. Pack a snack and choose a casual park visit if that suits your group. This is a regular recreation option, not a scheduled event.

The city directory does not publish operating hours or a general admission fee on this park page. Confirm access and any shelter or court reservation needs with Community Services at (951) 736-2241; listed facilities do not guarantee an open reserved space.
