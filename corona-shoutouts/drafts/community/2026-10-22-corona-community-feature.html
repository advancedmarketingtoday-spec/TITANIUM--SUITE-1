Corona Weekend Planner: Trails, Making and Music for October 23–25

A Saturday of outdoor learning, hands-on creativity and live music, plus a useful two-day community drop-off

Corona’s October 23–25 weekend offers a practical mix of outdoor learning, creative time and live music. Saturday starts with a ranger-led hike for registered families, continues with free makerspace hours at Corona Public Library, and closes with Max Weinberg’s Jukebox at Dos Lagos. A free two-day household waste drop-off adds a useful community stop.

Start Saturday on the trail or with a helpful household errand

Nocturnal Animals hike with Corona Park Rangers

Corona Park Rangers will lead a strenuous hike in the Green River area while discussing nocturnal wildlife found in Southern California and its place in the local habitat. The city asks participants to plan for at least two hours on the trail.

  • Date: Saturday, October 24, 2026
  • Check-in and start: Check-in at 7:30 a.m.; hike begins at 8:00 a.m. Pacific
  • Meeting area: Green River Ranch Trail area; registered hikers should follow the city confirmation email for the exact trailhead
  • Ages: Children 5 and older may join with a parent; anyone under 18 needs a parent-signed waiver
  • Bring: Water, sunscreen, a hat and slip-resistant hiking shoes; no sandals or dogs

The city said confirmation emails would be sent Wednesday, October 21. The live recreation catalog did not display remaining capacity when checked October 22, so residents who are not already registered should check the catalog before heading out.

Free household hazardous-waste drop-off

Riverside County residents can bring accepted household hazardous waste to the Corona City Hall parking lot at 400 S. Vicentia Avenue. The city lists the event for Saturday and Sunday, October 24–25, from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. both days.

The official guide lists paint, used oil, auto batteries, electronic waste, compact fluorescent lights and used injection needles among the accepted materials. Review the city’s current waste guide before loading the car so every item is prepared correctly.

Give teens and adults time to make something at the library

Corona Public Library’s Open Create session runs Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. in the Innovation Center and Makerspace. The library lists 3D printers, embroidery and sewing machines, and a Glowforge laser among the equipment available during the session.

  • Date and time: Saturday, October 24, 10:00 a.m.–1:30 p.m. Pacific
  • Location: Corona Public Library, 650 S. Main Street
  • Ages: 13 and older
  • Admission: No fee or registration requirement is stated on the event listing

The library’s posted Saturday hours are 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., giving visitors a little time to browse before closing. The library is closed Sunday.

Close Saturday with an audience-picked set at Dos Lagos

Max Weinberg’s Jukebox comes to Dos Lagos Amphitheater on Saturday night. The format lets the audience choose songs from a set list spanning four decades, giving the concert a participatory twist.

  • Date: Saturday, October 24, 2026
  • Doors and show: Doors open at 6:30 p.m.; showtime is 8:00 p.m. Pacific
  • Location: Dos Lagos Amphitheater, 2755 Lakeshore Drive
  • Tickets: Reserved seats must be purchased online through the official ticket page
  • Venue policy: No outside food, drinks or chairs

The venue does not publish an age restriction or base ticket price on the event page. Families should confirm those details and current seat availability in the live ticket selector before buying.
