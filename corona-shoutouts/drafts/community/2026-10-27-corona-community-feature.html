Peppermint Ridge: A Corona Community Built Around Lifelong Support

How local homes, personalized care and volunteer partnerships help adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities stay connected

Peppermint Ridge gives adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities a lasting home base in Corona, along with personalized support and community activities. Its current mission page lists seven homes on its Magnolia Avenue campus and five more in the city. Residents can help through volunteering, partnerships or the nonprofit’s current Build-A-Bench campaign.

A home base that changes with each resident

Peppermint Ridge describes its work as lifelong housing paired with support that changes as a resident’s needs change. Individual care plans can address daily living, health and personal goals, while the organization’s Alternative Day Program offers activities, relationships and connections beyond the home.

The mix of seven campus homes and five homes elsewhere in Corona is a useful local detail. It shows that the Ridge is both a place on Magnolia Avenue and a network woven into the city. The organization says continuity matters: when needs change, its goal is to keep residents connected to familiar people, routines and community.

One Corona address with a citywide circle of support

The City of Corona’s nonprofit directory lists Peppermint Ridge at 825 Magnolia Avenue, with the same mission and contact details published by the organization. Peppermint Ridge identifies itself as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 95-2409851.

Its current Build-A-Bench campaign gives that mission a practical shape. As checked on October 27, 2026, the campaign page showed $16,848.66 raised toward a $30,000 goal and 40 benches pledged toward a goal of 50. Community benches are listed at $250, personalized legacy benches at $500, and gifts of any amount are welcome.

Those figures can change as new gifts arrive, so anyone planning to contribute should confirm the latest total on the official Peppermint Ridge website. Groups interested in a future volunteer Build Day can contact Nicholas Sam at nsam@peppermintridge.org or call (951) 273-7320.

Three practical ways to take part

  • Volunteer when your schedule allows. The Get Involved page directs new volunteers to its CivicChamps portal. The page does not publish a fixed schedule or age requirement, so confirm the right opportunity before making plans.
  • Bring a workplace or community group. Peppermint Ridge lists sponsorships, workplace giving, employee engagement, in-kind support and group projects among its partnership options. Businesses and civic, faith or service groups can contact Viki Battaglia at vbattaglia@peppermintridge.org.
  • Support a specific campus need. The Build-A-Bench campaign accepts full bench sponsorships and smaller gifts. Its purpose is to add places where residents can sit, visit and take part in activities.

Before heading out, check the Corona Weather Hub. Local families can also follow the published daily MaxPreps sports briefing for Corona-area school athletics.
