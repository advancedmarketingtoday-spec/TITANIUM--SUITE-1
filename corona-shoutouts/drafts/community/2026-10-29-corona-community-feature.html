Corona Weekend Planner: Halloween Fun at Dos Lagos and Family Time Around Town

Four verified ideas for Friday, October 30 through Sunday, November 1, 2026

Corona families can choose a creative library hour, an indoor costume party and a large Saturday celebration at The Shops at Dos Lagos during the October 30–November 1 weekend. Sunday offers a slower option at Santana Park’s inclusive playground. Every scheduled activity below comes from a current organizer or city listing.

Friday: code at the library or enjoy an indoor costume party

The Corona Public Library’s Friday Coding Class gives children ages 6 to 12 an hour in the Maker Exchange. The city describes this space as a place for patrons to create, experiment, build and explore. The library calendar listed the October 30 class when checked on October 29.

  • Date and time: Friday, October 30, 2026, from 4:45 to 5:45 p.m. PDT
  • Place: Corona Public Library Maker Exchange, 650 South Main Street, Corona
  • Ages: 6 to 12
  • Admission and registration: The public listing does not state a fee or registration rule. Check the library calendar or call 951-279-3657 before leaving.

For a later outing, iPlayology at The Shops at Dos Lagos has a Hotel Transylvania Halloween Glow Party. The organizer lists interactive games, dancing, a DJ, playground time and costumes for children ages 1 to 10. Each paid child ticket includes admission for two adults.

  • Date and time: Friday, October 30, 2026, from 5:30 to 8 p.m. PDT
  • Place: iPlayology, 2785 Cabot Drive, Suite 145, Corona
  • Ages: Child tickets are listed for ages 1 to 10; adults attend with children
  • Tickets: The organizer’s purchase page was active on October 29. It displayed more than one price, so confirm the checkout total or call 951-603-3186.
  • Before you go: The organizer requires a current waiver. Children need grip socks, and adults need regular socks.

Saturday: Halloween takes over The Shops at Dos Lagos

The Shops at Dos Lagos has its main Halloween celebration on Saturday afternoon. The official listing includes trick-or-treating at participating stores, music, face painters, balloon twisters, a magician, vendors and a stilt walker.

  • Date and time: Saturday, October 31, 2026, from 4 to 7 p.m. PDT
  • Place: The Shops at Dos Lagos, 2780 Cabot Drive, Corona
  • Costume contest: Awards are listed for toddlers age 3 and younger, ages 4 and older, adults and families
  • Admission and registration: The official event page does not state a fee or advance-registration requirement
  • Helpful detail: Trick-or-treating continues while supplies last, and the organizer recommends arriving early

No separate performance at the Dos Lagos Amphitheater was listed for October 30 through November 1 when the venue calendar was checked. The Halloween celebration is the verified Dos Lagos event for this weekend.

Sunday: make the park the easy part of the weekend

Santana Park is a flexible Sunday choice after two scheduled days of activities. This is a regular city amenity, not a timed event. The City of Corona says its pirate-themed inclusive playground is open, and the park directory also lists picnic areas, covered shelters, a skate park, soccer and softball fields, restrooms and ADA access.

  • Place: Santana Park, 598 Santana Way, Corona
  • Plan: Playground time, a picnic or open recreation using the park’s regular amenities
  • Hours and reservations: The city directory does not publish operating hours or field availability. Call Corona Parks at 951-736-2241 if your plans depend on a specific facility.
