Corona Weekend Planner: Cars, Community Service, Theater and Dos Lagos Music

Verified plans for Friday, November 6 through Sunday, November 8, 2026

Corona’s November 6–8 weekend brings four verified ways to gather: youth theater downtown, a free Veterans Day car show, a registered volunteer morning at Brentwood Park and an assigned-seat tribute concert at Dos Lagos Amphitheater. Most activity lands Saturday, so families can choose a morning outing, afternoon show or evening concert.

Start Saturday by serving or celebrating

Brentwood Park planting and cleanup

The City of Corona’s volunteer morning begins with check-in at 7:30 a.m. Saturday, November 7. Planting and cleanup work runs from 8 to 10 a.m. at Brentwood Park, 1646 Dawn Ridge Drive. The city lists the opportunity as free and open to all ages.

  • Wear closed-toed shoes and clothes that can get dirty; jeans are recommended.
  • The city provides work gloves, safety vests and water.
  • Teens must be at least 14 to volunteer without a parent. Anyone under 18 needs a parent to sign the waiver.
  • The posted registration window closed at 5 p.m. Wednesday, November 4. Residents who did not register should call (951) 736-2241 before going; walk-up access is not promised.

Veterans Day Classic Car Show at Crossings at Corona

From 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. Saturday, the fifth annual car show fills Crossings at Corona, 2470 Tuscany Street. Spectator admission is free. The Corona Chamber listing includes classic cars, a live DJ, petting zoo, face painting, a balloon artist and children’s crafts.

No spectator registration or age limit is listed. The mix makes this an easy morning choice for families who want an open-house style outing while recognizing local veterans.

Two local stages carry the weekend into the evening

Christian Arts & Theatre opens two fall productions

CAT Corona gives families several chances to support young performers at 815 West Sixth Street. Annie Jr. plays Friday at 7 p.m., Saturday at 11 a.m. and Sunday at 2 p.m. Singin’ in the Rain has Saturday performances at 2 and 7 p.m.

The official season page lists tickets at $22 or $26, plus a $2 service fee. It does not publish a separate audience age restriction or a live seat count. Confirm the performance entrance and current availability through the official purchase link before heading downtown.

Dos Lagos hosts a Saturday night tribute concert

Rock With You: The Michael Jackson Experience comes to the Dos Lagos Entertainment Center & Amphitheater on Saturday, November 7. Doors open at 6 p.m. and the show begins at 7:30 p.m. at 2755 Lakeshore Drive, Corona.

The venue says this performance uses reserved assigned seating only; lawn, bleacher and upper seating are unavailable. Free on-site parking is listed. Prices and remaining seats are managed through the official Dos Lagos ticket page, and the event page does not state an age restriction.

Check the details before choosing your route

The Corona Public Library calendar was checked for November 6–8, but no separately scheduled weekend program was located. The library at 650 South Main Street lists regular Saturday hours from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. and is closed Sunday. Those are building hours, not a special event.

Outdoor plans can change, so check the Corona Weather Hub before leaving. Families can also browse the published Corona prep sports briefings for local school athletics.
