Corona Weekend Planner: Dos Lagos Cars, Library Making and Family Theater

Verified plans for Friday, November 13, through Sunday, November 15, 2026

Corona’s best verified plans for Friday, November 13, through Sunday, November 15, bring neighbors together at Dos Lagos, the Corona Public Library and CAT’s Main Stage. Saturday starts with cars and coffee, then shifts to hands-on learning and two family-friendly stage productions, with choices for early birds, teens and theater fans.

Start Saturday with Cars and Coffee at Dos Lagos

The Cars and Coffee Corona organizer lists its family-friendly meet every Saturday from 7 to 9 a.m. at The Shops at Dos Lagos. That places this weekend’s gathering on Saturday, November 14. Spectators are welcome, and the mix may include classics, muscle cars, off-road vehicles and newer luxury models.

  • Date and time: Saturday, November 14, 2026, 7–9 a.m. Pacific
  • Location: The Shops at Dos Lagos, 2780 Cabot Drive, Corona, CA 92883
  • Good to know: Parking is first come, first served. The organizer says the meet does not run when it rains and asks drivers to arrive and leave quietly.
  • Admission and registration: The organizer’s page does not list a price, registration requirement or minimum age.

This is a shopping-center gathering, not a Dos Lagos Amphitheater performance. The official Dos Lagos ticket calendar did not show a separate amphitheater event for November 13–15 when checked on November 12; its next listed November program is the November 28 tree lighting and holiday concert.

Make, learn and protect your phone at the library

Two Saturday programs give residents practical ways to learn at the Corona Public Library, 650 S. Main Street. Because their hours overlap, neighbors may want to choose the session that best fits their age and interests.

Phone Security & Privacy

This adult workshop covers phone and account protection, app permissions, online safety, and the difference between security and privacy. The library says learning materials are provided at no cost and registration is required.

  • Date and time: Saturday, November 14, 10 a.m.–noon Pacific
  • Listed location: Computer Learning Center
  • Audience: Adults; the registration form asks whether participants are age 16 or older
  • Availability: The official listing showed nine seats available when checked November 12
  • Registration: Use the library’s event page or call 951-279-3746

The page is labeled “In-Person” and names the Computer Learning Center, but it also contains an online-event note. Confirm the format during registration before making the trip.

Open Create in the Innovation Center

Open Create gives teens and adults time with makerspace equipment, including 3D printers, embroidery and sewing machines, and a Glowforge laser cutter. The official listing is for ages 13 and older.

  • Date and time: Saturday, November 14, 10 a.m.–1:30 p.m. Pacific
  • Location: Innovation Center / Makerspace
  • Audience: Teens and adults, ages 13+
  • Admission and registration: The event page does not state a price or registration requirement

Choose a family musical at CAT’s Main Stage

Christian Arts & Theatre of Corona closes its fall run with two family-friendly productions at 815 W. Sixth Street. Singin’ in the Rain offers a movie-musical story, while Annie Jr. brings a shorter youth-theater favorite to the same stage.

Singin’ in the Rain

  • Performances: Friday, November 13, 7 p.m.; Saturday, November 14, 2 p.m.; Sunday, November 15, 2 p.m. Pacific
  • Approximate runtime: 2 hours, 15 minutes

Annie Jr.

  • Performances: Saturday, November 14, 11 a.m. and 7 p.m. Pacific
  • Approximate runtime: 1 hour

Tickets for either production are listed at $22 or $26, plus a $2 service fee. Check CAT’s official season page for purchasing and current availability before leaving home.
