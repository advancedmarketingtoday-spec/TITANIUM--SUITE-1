Corona Weekend Planner: Coding, Cars and the First Tree Lighting

Verified family plans for Friday, November 20, through Sunday, November 22, 2026

Corona families can build a full weekend around hands-on learning, a Saturday morning car gathering at Dos Lagos and an evening tree lighting at The Crossings. The clearest choices run Friday and Saturday, with published times, locations and age guidance that make it easier to plan before leaving home.

Friday starts with coding and open making at the library

The Corona Public Library gives young learners a focused Friday afternoon option. Its Coding Class introduces coding and robotics for children ages 6 to 12 in the Computer Learning Center.

  • Date and time: Friday, November 20, from 4:45 to 5:45 p.m. Pacific
  • Location: Corona Public Library, 650 South Main Street, Corona
  • Age: Children ages 6 to 12
  • Before you go: The official listing does not state a price, registration requirement or remaining capacity. Families should check the event page before traveling.

Teens and adults ages 13 and older can also use the library’s Open Create time on Friday. The Innovation Center listing notes equipment that includes 3D printers, sewing and embroidery machines, and a Glowforge laser cutter.

  • Friday hours: 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. and 2 to 5:30 p.m. Pacific
  • Location: Innovation Center/Makerspace at the Corona Public Library
  • Age: 13 and older
  • Before you go: Price, registration and capacity are not stated on the official listing.

Saturday morning brings makerspace time and Dos Lagos cars

Open Create returns Saturday from 10 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. for ages 13 and older. It is a practical indoor option for residents who want to learn what the makerspace can do, but the library page does not state price, registration or current capacity.

Early risers can head to Cars and Coffee Corona at The Shops at Dos Lagos. The organizer says the family-friendly gathering runs every Saturday from 7 to 9 a.m., unless it is raining or Christmas, so the November 21 appearance follows that published weekly schedule.

  • Date and time: Saturday, November 21, from 7 to 9 a.m. Pacific
  • Location: The Shops at Dos Lagos, 2780 Cabot Drive, Corona
  • Access: Spectators are welcome; parking is first come, first served
  • Before you go: The organizer asks guests to arrive and leave quietly. Admission price, registration and a minimum age are not stated.

Saturday evening lights up at The Crossings

The weekend’s biggest scheduled celebration is the Tree Lighting and Santa’s Arrival at The Crossings at Corona. The center lists the event as free, with festivities from 5 to 7:30 p.m.; the Corona Chamber calendar says performances begin before the tree lighting at 6 p.m.

  • Date and time: Saturday, November 21, from 5 to 7:30 p.m. Pacific
  • Location: The Crossings at Corona, 2470 Tuscany Street, Corona
  • Admission: Free and open to the public
  • Activities: Family entertainment, children’s crafts, balloon artists, live performances, and photos with Santa and the Grinch
  • Age and registration: No age restriction or registration requirement is stated on the official venue listing

No separate Dos Lagos Amphitheater program was listed for November 20–22 on the center’s official event calendar; its next posted holiday activities begin November 27. We also could not verify a scheduled City of Corona park program for this weekend, so regular park amenities should not be mistaken for an organized event.
