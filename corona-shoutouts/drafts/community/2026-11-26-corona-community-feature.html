Corona Weekend Planner: Dos Lagos Lights Up Thanksgiving Weekend

Verified plans for Friday, November 27, through Sunday, November 29, 2026

Corona's holiday season opens this weekend with three verified choices: the new ticketed Christmas Light Show at Dos Lagos, Saturday's free tree-lighting concert in the central plaza and Open Create hours at Corona Public Library. Friday through Sunday plans are concentrated at Dos Lagos, while the library reopens Saturday after its holiday closure.

Opening Weekend at the Dos Lagos Christmas Light Show

The Dos Lagos Christmas Light Show is a walk-through experience built around a 40-foot center tree, a light tunnel, a maze, photo settings and a synchronized lighting program. The first synchronized show begins at 5:30 p.m. and repeats every 30 minutes.

  • Dates and hours: Friday, November 27, and Saturday, November 28, from 4:30 to 10 p.m.; Sunday, November 29, from 4:30 to 9 p.m.
  • Location: The Shops at Dos Lagos, 2780 Cabot Drive, Corona, CA 92883.
  • Admission: General admission starts at $20.99 Friday and Saturday and $15.99 Sunday. Every guest older than 2 needs a paid ticket; children age 2 and younger enter free.
  • Tickets: Advance online purchase is recommended. Walk-up admission may depend on capacity, and prices or availability can change.
  • Optional train: $10 per rider. Children age 2 and younger ride free while seated on an adult's lap.

The attraction is outdoors, so families may want to check the Corona Weather Hub before leaving home. Choosing a timed ticket in advance also makes it easier to plan dinner or shopping elsewhere at Dos Lagos.

Saturday's Free Concert and Tree Lighting

The Dos Lagos Annual Tree Lighting & Holiday Concert brings a second Saturday option to the same shopping center. This event is in the central shopping plaza on a temporary stage, not at Dos Lagos Amphitheater.

  • Date: Saturday, November 28, 2026.
  • Schedule: Holiday Night Market at 4 p.m.; free concert at 5 p.m.; tree lighting at 7 p.m.; market closes at 8 p.m.
  • Entertainment: Boy Band All Stars, Break Free Ariana Grande Tribute, the Santiago High School Marching Band and Santa.
  • Admission: General admission is free and does not require a ticket. The organizer lists optional VIP floor seating at $25 per unassigned seat, first come, first served, with 200 seats available.
  • Location: Central plaza at The Shops at Dos Lagos, 2780 Cabot Drive, Corona, CA 92883.

The event page does not list an age restriction. Families who want both Dos Lagos activities should note that the free concert and tree lighting are separate from the ticketed light-show experience.

Saturday Makerspace Hours and Honest Calendar Gaps

Open Create gives teens and adults time to explore the Corona Public Library's Innovation Center. The listed equipment includes 3D printers, sewing and embroidery machines, and a Glowforge laser printer.

  • Date and time: Saturday, November 28, from 10 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.
  • Location: Innovation Center/Makerspace, Corona Public Library, 650 S. Main Street, Corona, CA 92882.
  • Ages: 13 and older; the library lists the audience as teens and adults.
  • Registration and cost: The official listing does not state a fee, registration requirement or remaining capacity. Check the listing before traveling.

The library is closed Friday, November 27, for the Thanksgiving holiday and resumes its listed Saturday hours. No separate Dos Lagos Amphitheater program and no scheduled City of Corona park event for November 27-29 appeared on the official calendars reviewed November 26; parks remain useful as regular amenities, but they are not being presented here as scheduled events.
