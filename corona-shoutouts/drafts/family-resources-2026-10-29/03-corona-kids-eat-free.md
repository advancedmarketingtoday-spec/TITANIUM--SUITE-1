# Kids Eat Free (or Cheap) in Corona: A Night-by-Night Guide

Corona families have two useful weekday starting points: Habit Burger & Grill advertises a qualifying free kids meal Monday–Thursday through MyHabit, and El Torito advertises $1 kids meals on Wednesdays with an adult entrée. Both come with conditions. Weekend deals and other chains’ local participation need separate confirmation.

A good dinner deal should survive the checkout screen. This guide separates the offers supported by current restaurant pages from the leads that still require a call, so a “free” meal does not become a surprise at the register.

The Habit entries describe **one offer usable on a choice of weekdays**, not four free meals per member each week. That distinction makes the calendar useful without overstating the savings.

## Monday through Thursday: understand the Habit offer

Habit’s [official Kids Eat Free page](https://www.habitburger.com/kidseatfree/) lists **Corona and North Corona** among participating locations. It describes one free Kids Favorite Meal with a qualifying purchase of at least **$10**, available to **MyHabit members** Monday through Thursday.

The detailed terms cover children **10 and under**, with eligible meals including Kids Chicken Bites, Kids Charburger and Kids Grilled Cheese. Cheese and other add-ons may cost extra. Because one short description on the page uses different age wording, confirm the offer shown in your wallet for a child who is exactly 10.

The published promotion dates run through **January 1, 2027**, and the page calls it a limited-time offer. Each member gets **one redemption per week**. A Monday visit uses that week’s offer; the same member cannot treat Tuesday as a fresh redemption.

The terms require a logged-in order on Habit’s website or app and a valid account with an accepted payment method. Do not assume showing a screenshot at a counter will work. Select the restaurant, add the qualifying items, apply the offer, and confirm the discount before paying.

Habit’s [Corona location page](https://www.habitburger.com/locations/corona-dt/) lists **1987 Foothill Boulevard** and **951-460-1010**. Check the selected store in the app, particularly if you are deciding between Corona and a nearby city. Some neighboring locations appear on the promotion’s exclusion list.

## Wednesday: El Torito’s $1 kids meal

El Torito’s [Wednesday offer](https://www.eltorito.com/kidswednesdays/) states that children **12 and under** can get a kids meal for **$1** with purchase of an adult entrée. The advertised offer is available all day Wednesday.

The [Corona restaurant](https://www.eltorito.com/location/corona/) is at **2240 Griffin Way**, with telephone **951-736-9379**. Call if you need to confirm the number of discounted kids meals allowed for your adult purchases, exclusions, or whether your preferred ordering method qualifies.

The adult entrée is part of the cost calculation. Compare the full family order rather than choosing a restaurant solely because the child’s plate is inexpensive. Drinks, premium choices, tax and gratuity may change the final total.

If Wednesday is already a busy practice night, establish the terms before everyone is hungry. A quick question—“Does the kids Wednesday offer apply to our order today?”—is easier to resolve before ordering than after the bill arrives.

## Denny’s is a lead to call, not a confirmed Tuesday promise

Denny’s [official FAQ](https://dennys.com/faqs-frequently-asked-questions) describes Kids Eat Free at select restaurants: up to two qualifying kids entrées with an adult entrée costing at least $6, for dine-in. That national information does not establish the participating hours or days at a particular Corona restaurant.

Official location pages identify Denny’s at [1833 W. Sixth Street](https://dennys.com/location/denny-s-7568-82005) and [2379 Compton Avenue](https://dennys.com/location/denny-s-7715-81801). Contact the restaurant you plan to visit and ask whether it currently participates, which day and hours apply, and the eligible ages.

If staff confirms an offer, note the location and confirmation date in your own dinner plan. Until then, do not build a Tuesday outing around an old neighborhood list. Franchise participation and local schedules can differ even within the same chain.

## Friday through Sunday: compare the whole order

We did not establish a current recurring Corona child-meal promotion for these three days. That is a reporting gap, not evidence that no restaurant has one. It is also a reason not to fill the calendar with offers from another city.

For a weekend meal, compare the actual menu for your selected restaurant and check official app offers. Look at the food your children will eat, portion sizes, side choices and drink prices. A lower total can matter more than the word “free.”

Watch the expiration date on every promotion page. During research, an Applebee’s free-kids-meal page still accessible online turned out to contain **August 2022** terms. A live webpage is not proof that an offer is current, and that promotion is excluded here.

## Save this checklist with the calendar

Before leaving, confirm the restaurant address, eligible age, required adult purchase, redemption method, and number of children covered. Ask whether the deal can combine with another coupon. If ordering online, review the final amount before submitting payment.

For dietary restrictions, ask the restaurant directly about ingredients and preparation. A discount should never be the reason to skip a necessary food-safety question. Choose another option if the staff cannot answer what your family needs to know.

Save a screenshot of the current terms for your own reference, but use the restaurant’s live offer to redeem. If you find another Corona deal, send Corona Shoutouts the restaurant’s official link and location so it can be checked before being added.
