## Editorial checks before live publication

- Restaurant guide: Habit and El Torito offers have current primary-source support. Denny's local days and participation remain unconfirmed. No recurring Friday–Sunday Corona offer was verified; the article says so explicitly. Recheck promotion terms at publication, particularly Habit's January 1, 2027 end date.
- Library guide: Corona state and county park-pass lending is verified. Museum-ticket eligibility through a Corona card is not verified. Do not turn the headline or promotional copy into a promise of free museums with a Corona card.
- Swim guide: YMCA currently lists Saturday lessons at Corona High and Roosevelt in Eastvale. October–December 2026 session dates need refreshing if publication is delayed. Exact class age cutoffs, prices and spaces require registration confirmation.
- Disability guide: Inclusive playgrounds at Lincoln and Santana and adaptive recreation are verified. No specific current sensory-friendly event is invented or promised. Some adaptive activities start at 14 and require a caregiver.
- Teen guide: Library opportunities have role-specific age rules. Corona shelter volunteers must be 18 or older. Employer links are application routes; no unverified teen vacancy or internship is claimed.
- College guide: 2027–28 FAFSA availability and March 2, 2027 California priority date have official-source support. No upcoming CNUSD help-night date was verified. Norco scholarship windows are stated at the month level shown by the source; exact closing dates should be checked in the live portal.
- Car seat guide: Law checked for October 29, 2026; reverify for publication in 2027 or later. CHP is the verified contact route. No walk-in Corona fire-station program is claimed. Appointment availability and any cost must be confirmed directly.
- 4-H guide: County describes ages 5–19. Enrollment pages retain old 2024–25 pricing; no stale fee is quoted as current. Current club contacts, projects, fees and age-year rules require direct confirmation.
- Emergency guide: Preparedness article, not a live incident bulletin. Use official current alerts for an actual incident.
- Assistance guide: Funding and approvals are not guaranteed. City of Riverside SHARE is distinguished from county LIHEAP and Corona water-bill questions.
