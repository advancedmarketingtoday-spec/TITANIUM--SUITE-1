# Commuting from Corona: Compare the 91, I-15 and Metrolink

*Check the whole journey at your work hours before choosing an address.*

A Corona commute can involve driving, rail or a combination of trips. The useful comparison starts at your front door and ends at your destination, including parking, waiting and transfers.

This guide does not promise a fixed travel time to Los Angeles, Orange County or another workplace. A route that looks manageable during a home tour may work differently on the days and hours you expect to commute.

### Build a driving trial

For a candidate home, identify the route you would actually take toward the 91 or I-15. Test both directions at relevant times when practical, and keep dated observations.

Include the local-street portion, workplace parking and the walk from the parking area. If you are comparing two homes, use the same destinations and similar travel windows so the observations are useful.

Consider what you would do when your usual route has a closure or delay. A fallback plan is more informative than selecting the fastest result from a single map search.

### Treat express lanes as a separate budget choice

The [91 Express Lanes operator](https://www.91expresslanes.com/) publishes toll and account information. Review the current rules for your route and vehicle before using the lanes.

Do not assume the 91 and I-15 have identical tolls, discounts or entry arrangements. Use the relevant operator’s information for each segment and put any expected toll use in your [household budget](cost-of-living-corona-california.md).

This guide leaves rates out because the amount depends on the segment and current schedule. An operator’s published price is the source to consult for the trip you plan to make.

### Compare the two Corona rail stations

The official [Corona–North Main station page](https://metrolinktrains.com/rider-info/general-info/stations/corona---north-main/) gives its address as **250 E. Blaine St., Corona, CA 92879** and lists the **91/Perris Valley** and **Inland Empire–Orange County** lines.

Metrolink also maintains a [Corona–West station page](https://metrolinktrains.com/rider-info/general-info/stations/corona---west/). Compare the station that fits your home route with the destination and current train timetable, rather than choosing solely by which station is closest.

Check the official schedule and service advisories for outbound and return trips. Add the journey to the station, parking or drop-off, any transfer and the final connection to work. Confirm ticket and boarding requirements before a trial ride.

### Test the routine you can sustain

Write down door-to-door time, total cost, reliability observations and the practical constraints of each option. If your work hours change, repeat the comparison for those hours.

Use the [neighborhood guide](corona-neighborhoods-and-city-boundaries.md) to connect the results with your address shortlist. Return to the [moving guide](moving-to-corona-california.md) once travel and housing costs are in the same worksheet.

The next step is a realistic trial, not a universal ranking of Corona commutes. Choose the option that fits the schedule you actually keep.
