# Corona Schools and Enrollment: A Guide for Moving Families

*Verify the district and school assignment before building the rest of your family’s schedule.*

For a family moving to Corona, school research begins with an address. A listing’s school names can be helpful leads, but they should not replace confirmation from the district.

The [City’s school district resource](https://www.coronaca.gov/community/getting-involved/school-districts) links **Corona-Norco Unified School District** and **Alvord Unified School District**. Check which district serves your home before using an individual school locator or starting an application.

### Use the current school-year tool

For an address confirmed within CNUSD, the [district boundary page](https://www.cnusd.k12.ca.us/departments/business_services/facilities/boundaries) presents a **2026–27 School Year Boundary Map using School Locator**. Use the current-year link on that page and enter the actual address.

Record the result and the date you checked it. If an address is new, difficult to locate or near a boundary, contact district staff. A missing map result is a question to resolve, not permission to choose an assignment yourself.

Attendance boundaries and transfer arrangements are different matters. If you want a school other than the address-based assignment, ask the district about the current process and availability. This guide does not promise that a transfer will be approved.

### Organize enrollment without guessing the requirements

CNUSD publishes a [pre-enrollment page](https://www.cnusd.k12.ca.us/parents/enrollment/pre-_enrollment). Start with the district’s current instructions and ask the school how to complete the process.

Prepare to discuss the student’s grade, prior school records, residency documentation and any support needs, but use the school’s actual document checklist. Do not assume a checklist from a previous district or school year remains sufficient.

Keep copies of what you submit and confirmation of the next step. If you are moving during the school year, ask how the school handles the anticipated start date and any records still being transferred.

### Ask about the daily schedule

Verify the school calendar, start and finish times, transportation eligibility and after-school arrangements. Work out who will handle arrival, pickup and unexpected schedule changes.

Our [commuting guide](commuting-from-corona-91-15-metrolink.md) can help you test the combined home-school-work trip. Budget separately for any childcare or transport you expect to arrange.

Ask about programs directly instead of assuming every campus offers the same options. For services a student needs, discuss the situation with appropriate school staff early enough to plan the transition.

### Learn about the community beyond a rating

A single online score cannot answer every question about a school’s fit for a student. Use district information, conversations and visits where available to understand the programs and routines relevant to your family.

Our [youth sports coverage](https://coronashoutouts.com/articles/youth-sports-leagues) offers community context; it is not an enrollment or eligibility source. The [library](https://www.coronaca.gov/departments/library) is another official resource to explore after your move.

Return to the [moving guide](moving-to-corona-california.md) when the assignment and enrollment steps are confirmed. Put the school’s current instructions ahead of assumptions from a housing advertisement.
