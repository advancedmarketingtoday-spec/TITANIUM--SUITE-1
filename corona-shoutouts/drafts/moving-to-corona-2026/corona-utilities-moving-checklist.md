# Corona Utilities Checklist: Set Up Services Before Moving Day

*Confirm each provider by address and keep the start dates in one place.*

Utilities are easier to arrange before the moving truck arrives. Begin with the exact address, the date you take possession and any service responsibilities described in the lease or purchase documents.

A Corona postal address does not prove that one provider handles every service. Confirm water, electricity, gas, waste collection and internet separately.

### Start with water service

The [City’s start/stop service page](https://www.coronaca.gov/departments/utilities/customer-care/start-stop-services) directs new water customers to Utilities Customer Care at **951-736-2321** or an in-person visit. Ask whether your address is served by the City and what is needed to establish the account.

Confirm the effective date and any fees. The page describes setup and after-hours charges, so leave time to arrange activation rather than assuming it is immediate or free.

Keep the confirmation in your moving folder. For a rental, ask management whether you open the account or pay through another documented arrangement. Avoid opening an account based only on a general neighborhood description.

### Identify the electricity provider

The [City’s new-resident page](https://www.coronaca.gov/community/getting-involved/new-residents) says most Corona residents receive electricity from Southern California Edison, while certain areas receive City electric service.

The City’s start/stop page specifically asks electric customers to verify its service area before applying. Follow that address check instead of assuming proximity to a named shopping center decides your provider.

If another provider serves the home, use that provider’s official setup instructions. Record the account holder, service date and confirmation independently from water service.

### Check gas, trash and internet

Ask the property manager or seller which gas and waste providers currently serve the address, then confirm with those providers. Clarify who receives each bill and which charges are included in your housing payment.

For waste collection, ask about pickup day, bins, recycling rules and arrangements for moving boxes or large items. Use the current instructions rather than leaving extra material at the curb without confirmation.

For internet, verify service to the specific unit, not only the street. Ask about installation access, equipment, pricing after any introductory period and cancellation terms. This guide does not endorse a provider or promise that advertised speeds are available at your home.

### Put dates and responsibilities in one table

For each service, record the provider, contact link, account holder, requested start date, confirmed start date and any open question. Do the same for stopping services at the home you are leaving.

The [City’s rates and services page](https://www.coronaca.gov/departments/utilities/customer-care/services) is a starting point for applicable municipal charges. Our [cost-of-living guide](cost-of-living-corona-california.md) helps separate recurring bills from startup expenses.

Once services are confirmed, follow the [first-30-days guide](first-30-days-in-corona.md) for the remaining local tasks. Return to the [moving guide](moving-to-corona-california.md) if an address or possession date changes; those changes may affect the whole setup schedule.
