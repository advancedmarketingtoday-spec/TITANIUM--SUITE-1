# Your First 30 Days in Corona: A New Resident Checklist

*Handle the time-sensitive tasks early, then build a routine one week at a time.*

Your first month in Corona does not need to be a race to learn everything. Use this four-week plan to organize essential tasks and leave space for a few local discoveries.

The week-by-week order below is an editorial suggestion. Official deadlines, your school’s instructions and the dates in your housing documents take priority.

### Week one: confirm the essentials

Check that the services you arranged are active and that the billing responsibilities match your agreement. Save confirmations together with your [utilities checklist](corona-utilities-moving-checklist.md).

Walk through the home and document questions or issues promptly through the appropriate management or transaction channel. Learn how to contact the responsible provider when something needs attention.

If you have students, follow up on the enrollment steps in our [schools guide](corona-schools-enrollment-guide.md). Confirm the actual start date, arrival arrangements and any unfinished records transfer.

For an interstate move, consult the [California DMV’s new-resident portal](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-education-and-safety/special-interest-driver-guides/new-to-california/) right away. Its vehicle-registration guidance includes a 20-day requirement for vehicles brought from another state or country; check the instructions that apply to your situation rather than waiting until the end of this plan.

### Week two: learn your local resources

Bookmark the [City’s new-resident page](https://www.coronaca.gov/community/getting-involved/new-residents) and the official resources for your jurisdiction. It provides starting points for city services, animal services and home-improvement questions.

If you have a pet, check the responsible animal-services office’s current licensing requirements. Before starting alterations, ask the appropriate building or planning office about permits instead of assuming a small project is exempt.

Visit [Corona Public Library](https://www.coronaca.gov/departments/library) at **650 South Main Street** when its current hours fit your schedule. Check its official page for card requirements and program information.

### Week three: test and adjust your routine

Review the commute you have actually experienced. Use our [commuting guide](commuting-from-corona-91-15-metrolink.md) if you want to test a different option.

Compare the first bills and receipts with your [moving budget](cost-of-living-corona-california.md). Update estimates where actual costs differ, and keep one-time moving expenses separate from the ongoing household total.

Choose a nearby outdoor stop through our [parks guide](corona-parks-trails-new-residents.md). A short visit can be enough while you are still unpacking.

### Week four: find a comfortable community connection

Browse the [Corona Shoutouts event calendar](https://www.coronashoutouts.com/events) and verify any activity with its organizer. Choose an event, library program or volunteer opportunity that fits your interests and schedule.

Check the [shopping and dining guide](corona-shopping-dining-new-residents.md) for a first local outing, and read our [Grand Boulevard history](https://coronashoutouts.com/a/corona-history-grand-boulevard-road-races) for background.

Return to the [moving guide](moving-to-corona-california.md) when a larger question remains. By the end of the month, aim for confirmed services, a workable routine and a small set of reliable local resources. The rest can grow as you settle in.
