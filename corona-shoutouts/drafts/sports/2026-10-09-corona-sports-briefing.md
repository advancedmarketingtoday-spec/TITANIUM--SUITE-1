## Corona High Panthers

Corona’s teams put in a busy Thursday on the road and at home. The varsity volleyball Panthers visited Roosevelt, where Roosevelt won in three sets. Corona’s JV and freshman teams also played at Roosevelt, with both matches ending 2-0 for the hosts. The results move the varsity record to 12-20 overall and 0-9 in the Big VIII; JV is 8-11 overall and 2-5 in league, while the freshman team is 3-12 and 0-4.

- **Thursday finals:** Girls varsity volleyball at Roosevelt, 0-3; girls JV volleyball at Roosevelt, 0-2; girls freshman volleyball at Roosevelt, 0-2.
- **Thursday final:** Boys varsity water polo hosted Norco, with Norco earning a 10-6 result. Corona is 15-13 overall and 1-5 in Big VIII play.
- **Thursday final:** Girls varsity tennis hosted Centennial. MaxPreps marks Corona the winner after a 9-9 team score; the feed does not explain the tiebreak, so that detail needs confirmation before publication.
- **Friday:** Varsity football visits Roosevelt at **7 p.m. PT**. Corona enters 4-2 overall and 0-1 in the Big West-South, listed fifth in the feed.
- **Friday:** Boys varsity water polo is listed against Paloma Valley at **1 p.m. PT** at an unverified site.

## Centennial High Huskies

Centennial’s volleyball program had a strong home evening against King. The varsity Huskies earned a 3-0 result and improved to 27-10 overall and 9-0 in the Big VIII, where MaxPreps lists them first. The JV team added a 2-0 result and stands 7-2 in league, listed second. No final was posted for the freshman match by the end of this reporting window.

- **Thursday finals:** Girls varsity volleyball defeated King 3-0; girls JV volleyball defeated King 2-0.
- **Thursday final:** Girls varsity tennis played at Corona. MaxPreps marks Centennial with a loss after a 9-9 team score; the deciding tiebreak is not shown.
- **Friday:** Varsity football hosts Murrieta Valley at **7 p.m. PT**. Centennial is 3-3 overall and 1-0 in the Big West-North, listed first.
- **Friday:** Boys varsity water polo has conflicting MaxPreps tournament listings at **2 p.m. PT**, one at Charter Oak and another at Mission Viejo. Opponent and venue require confirmation.
