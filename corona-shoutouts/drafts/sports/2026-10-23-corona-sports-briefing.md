Corona hosts Temecula Valley and Centennial visits Chaparral in Friday's two verified local varsity football games. ## Thursday's football results

Norco's varsity team earned a 42-30 home win over Vista Murrieta. Centennial freshman football defeated Chaparral 55-34, while the Centennial JV game finished 42-41 in Chaparral's favor.

Santiago varsity football finished its road league game at Roosevelt with a 35-0 result. Corona's two lower-level teams played at Temecula Valley, with the JV game ending 38-16 and the freshman game 21-0, both in favor of the home team.

## Corona Panthers

Corona returns home tonight for a Big West South game against Temecula Valley. MaxPreps now lists Corona at 5-3 overall and 1-2 in league, fifth. The page changed the October 2 King result to a 35-21 forfeit win, which explains the record change from earlier listings.

- **Thursday results:** JV football fell 38-16 at Temecula Valley. Freshman football fell 21-0 at Temecula Valley.
- **Friday matchup:** Varsity football hosts Temecula Valley at 7:00 p.m. MaxPreps lists Corona as the home team at 1150 West Tenth Street in Corona.
- **Current records:** Varsity 5-3 overall, 1-2 league, fifth. JV 4-5 overall, 0-3 league. Freshman 1-7 overall, 0-1 league, fifth.
