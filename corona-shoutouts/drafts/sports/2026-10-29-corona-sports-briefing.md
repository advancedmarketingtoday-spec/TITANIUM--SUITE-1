# Corona sports briefing: Four varsity football games lead Thursday’s schedule

*Corona, Centennial, Santiago and Norco all play varsity football Thursday, while Santiago and Norco also have boys water-polo matches.*

Thursday brings six verified events for the four local schools: four varsity football games and two boys water-polo matches. Wednesday’s lower-level football schedule produced five confirmed finals, including two Centennial wins and a Santiago freshman win. Santiago’s JV result at King had not been posted by early Thursday morning.

## Centennial Huskies

Centennial’s freshman and JV football teams both earned home wins over Murrieta Mesa on Wednesday. The freshman Huskies won 31–14, bringing their record to 7-3 overall and 5-0 in the Big West Upper, first in the league. The JV team won 55–7 and is now 8-2 overall and 4-1 in league, second.

The varsity Huskies visit Murrieta Mesa tonight. MaxPreps lists the start time as 7 p.m., although Wednesday’s directory preview displayed a conflicting 7:30 p.m. time before the team schedule updated. Manual confirmation with the host is recommended.

- **Wednesday finals:** Freshman football defeated Murrieta Mesa 31–14; JV football defeated Murrieta Mesa 55–7.
- **Thursday matchup:** Varsity football at Murrieta Mesa, listed for 7:00 p.m. PT, away.
- **Varsity standing:** 6-3 overall and 4-0 in the Big West North, first.

## Corona Panthers

Corona’s freshman and JV teams completed their home games with Great Oak on Wednesday. Great Oak won the freshman game 48–6 and the JV game 50–14. The Panthers now turn their attention to tonight’s varsity road game at Great Oak.

- **Wednesday finals:** Freshman football fell to Great Oak 48–6; JV football fell to Great Oak 50–14.
- **Thursday matchup:** Varsity football at Great Oak, 7:00 p.m. PT, away.
- **Varsity standing:** 5-4 overall and 1-3 in the Big West South, fifth.

## Santiago Sharks

Santiago’s freshman team won 13–6 at King on Wednesday. The Sharks’ JV game also appears on the schedule, but the feed showed “View Matchup” without a final score, so that result remains unverified.

Thursday is a two-sport day for Santiago. Varsity football hosts King at Santiago High School, 1395 Foothill Parkway in Corona. Boys varsity water polo travels to El Segundo for a listed 5 p.m. playoff match.

- **Wednesday final:** Freshman football defeated King 13–6. JV score unreported.
- **Thursday matchups:** Boys varsity water polo at El Segundo, 5:00 p.m. PT, away, playoff; varsity football vs. King, 7:00 p.m. PT, home.
- **Current records:** Football is 6-3 overall and 1-3 in the Big West South, fourth. Water polo is 16-9 overall and 5-3 in the Big VIII, third.

## Norco Cougars

Murrieta Valley won both Wednesday lower-level football games at Norco, taking the freshman contest 14–0 and the JV game 36–16. The Cougars have two verified events today, with boys water polo at home and varsity football on the road.

- **Wednesday finals:** Freshman football fell to Murrieta Valley 14–0; JV football fell to Murrieta Valley 36–16.
- **Thursday matchups:** Boys varsity water polo vs. Burbank at 5:00 p.m. PT, home; varsity football at Murrieta Valley at 7:00 p.m. PT, away.
- **Current records:** Football is 6-3 overall and 2-2 in the Big West North, fourth. Water polo is 20-8 overall and 8-2 in the Big VIII, second.
