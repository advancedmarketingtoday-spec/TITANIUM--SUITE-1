# Corona Sports Today: Santiago Sweeps League Cross-Country Titles; Norco Water Polo Sets Quarterfinal

Santiago runners delivered Wednesday's biggest local performances, winning both varsity races at the Big VIII League cross-country finals in Eastvale. The next playoff spotlight belongs to Norco boys water polo, Norco football and Santiago football, all scheduled to compete Friday.

## Last Night's Results

Santiago sophomore Addyson Johns won the girls varsity three-mile race at the Big VIII League Finals in 16 minutes, 24.1 seconds on Wednesday at Eastvale Community Park. Teammates Avery Landi, Brooklyn Rodriguez, Malea Espinoza and Miranda Ibarra also finished among the first six runners, according to the published results. Eleanor Roosevelt's Emily Naslund placed 11th in 18:52.5, while Corona's Elisha Sedano led the Panthers in 26th at 21:16.6. The result sheet confirms individual places and times but did not display an official team-score table in the reviewed public view.

Santiago sophomore Jackson Horvath won the boys varsity three-mile race in 15:03.8 at the same league championship. Santiago's Lucas Heintz finished second in 15:30.4, Centennial's Gavin Salas placed third in 15:31.0, and Eleanor Roosevelt runners Sushanth Sashikumar and Logan Leal took fifth and sixth. Norco's Daniel Silva Jr. placed 22nd in 16:53.6, while Corona's Caleb Shin was 24th in 17:10.7. These are official individual marks from the meet results; team standings were not added because the available page did not show verified totals.

Santiago filled the first six places in the girls JV three-mile race. Violet King led in 19:32.4, followed by Diana Morales, Samantha Taras, Sophia Ibrahim, Anna Hill and Bailey Phelps. The reviewed result did not include scored-team totals.

Girls tennis also opened CIF Southern Section team playoffs Wednesday. Village Christian publicly reported a first-round win over Corona, but the announcement did not give a final team score, so it is not presented here as a complete result. Final scores for Santiago against Placentia Valencia and Eleanor Roosevelt against Burbank were not confirmed in the public sources reviewed before deadline.

## Players and Teams to Know

Addyson Johns earns today's first individual shoutout after her 16:24.1 league-title run. The result was published by the official meet timing source and MileSplit, so her name and mark are appropriate to recognize. Her finish led a deep Santiago girls varsity group that occupied six of the first eight places.

Jackson Horvath answered with the boys varsity title in 15:03.8. Lucas Heintz made it a Santiago one-two finish, while Centennial's Gavin Salas crossed only six-tenths of a second later in third. Those three local runners gave Corona-area families plenty to applaud at Eastvale Community Park.

The Norco boys water polo team carries a 22-8 published record into Friday's CIF Southern Section Division 3 quarterfinal. Norco reached this round with Tuesday's 12-10 victory over Camarillo. La Serna enters at 15-14 after a 9-5 playoff win over Millikan, making Friday's match a meeting of two teams coming off verified postseason victories.
