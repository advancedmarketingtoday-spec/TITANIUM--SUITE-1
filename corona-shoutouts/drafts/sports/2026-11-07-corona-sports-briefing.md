# Corona Sports Today: Santiago Football Advances and Norco Water Polo Moves On

Santiago football and Norco boys water polo both earned another week of playoff competition Friday, giving local families two strong reasons to celebrate. Saturday turns the spotlight to soccer, with four AYSO Area 1R championship matches at Santana Park and a full morning of club games at SilverLakes in Norco.

## Last Night's Results

Santiago’s varsity football team defeated Elsinore 31–14 in the CIF Southern Section Division 8 first round Friday night at Santiago High School in Corona. MaxPreps and the CIF scoreboard both list the game as final, and the Los Angeles Times playoff score roundup confirms the same result. The victory sends the Sharks into the quarterfinals, where they are scheduled to host Santa Monica at 7 p.m. Friday, November 13. Santiago entered the bracket as the No. 3 seed and now gets another home game.

Norco boys water polo beat La Serna 12–8 in Friday’s CIF Southern Section Division III playoff match, according to the updated MaxPreps scoreboard. The postseason run began with a 14–9 win over Burbank and a 12–10 victory over Camarillo. MaxPreps labeled Friday’s contest as neutral but did not publish the pool name. The Cougars’ next opponent, date and pool were not yet posted early Saturday.

Long Beach Poly defeated Norco 39–17 in the CIF Southern Section Division 3 football first round at Norco High School. The Jackrabbits move on to face Edison, while Norco’s public schedule showed no additional game Saturday morning. The Cougars close their season after earning a home playoff appearance. MaxPreps reports the final in its recap and Friday results.

## Players and Teams to Know

The Santiago football team earns the first shoutout today. The Sharks answered a difficult final stretch of league play with a 17-point playoff victory, and the published bracket now places them among the eight remaining Division 8 teams. No verified individual stat line was available in the public recap, so this recognition belongs to the full team.

Norco boys water polo also deserves a team-wide salute. Three postseason wins have carried the Cougars from the opening round into the next stage of Division III, with Friday’s defense holding La Serna to eight goals. The available score feed did not publish individual scorers or saves, so no individual performance is being assigned here.

Santiago cross country runners Jackson Horvath and Lucas Heintz remain worth knowing after Wednesday’s Big VIII boys varsity championship. Racecast lists Horvath first over three miles in 15:03.8 and Heintz second in 15:30.4, helping Santiago win the team title with 32 points, 15 ahead of Eleanor Roosevelt. Those results were published by the event-results provider and are separate from Friday’s playoff action.

## Eastvale Soccer

AYSO Area 1R has four championship finals at Santana Park in Corona, with two divisions starting at 5 p.m. and two at 7 p.m. These are match kickoff times, not an event-opening time. Both time slots have simultaneous games.

- **5 p.m., Field 5:** Boys 16U final, Perris No. 1 vs. Murrieta.
- **5 p.m., Field 6:** Girls 16U final, Riverside vs. Murrieta.
- **7 p.m., Field 5:** Boys 19U final, Corona vs. Moreno Valley.
- **7 p.m., Field 6:** Girls 19U final, Corona No. 2 vs. Menifee.

SilverLakes Sports Complex at 5555 Hamner Avenue in Norco also has verified club fixtures Saturday. Modular11 lists LAIFC against Rangers FC in a boys U15 Elite Academy match at 8 a.m. on Field 4. Total Futbol Academy has boys matches against Phoenix Rising at 9 a.m. for U16 and 11 a.m. for U14, both on Field 2. LA Surf Futures meets TFA at 9 a.m. in U17 on Field 23 and at 1 p.m. in U15 on Field 24.

Two more TFA matches are listed at SilverLakes Sunday: U16 against City SC Southwest at 9 a.m. and U14 against City SC Southwest at 1 p.m., both on Field 8. No reviewed source showed a separate tournament opening time, an Empire Soccer team assignment or a field-closure notice.

Eleanor Roosevelt’s high school soccer season has not started in the public schedule feeds reviewed for this roundup. The school’s MaxPreps directory confirms boys and girls soccer are offered, but no Saturday Mustangs match was published. That keeps today’s verified Eastvale-area soccer focus on the AYSO finals and SilverLakes club schedule.

## Around the Community

The City of Norco is accepting registration for its coed youth basketball league through November 16. The program serves ages 4–13 and costs $115 for residents or $125 for nonresidents. Practices begin November 30, with Saturday games starting January 9 at Riley Gymnasium; teams also need volunteer coaches.

Corona’s Circle City winter youth basketball registration remains open through Friday, November 13 for ages 4–14. The city’s fall program guide lists mandatory skills testing that day and practices beginning the week of January 4, 2027. Families should register through the City of Corona and confirm space before attending testing.

Eastvale-Jurupa NJB said Friday that winter registration continues only while spaces remain. Its November 6 update listed openings in several divisions and mandatory evaluations Sunday at Riley’s Gym, 3900 Acacia Avenue in Norco. Corona-Norco NJB also lists Sunday evaluations for its coed K–8 and high school leagues, with girls-only evaluations scheduled for November 15. Because openings can change quickly, families should check the appropriate league page before making the trip.

Good luck to every athlete taking the field, court or pool this weekend. Corona Shoutouts is cheering for the players, coaches, officials, volunteers and families who keep local sports moving with care and community spirit.
