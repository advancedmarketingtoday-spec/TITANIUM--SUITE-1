### Weekend recap — Friday, November 6, and Saturday, November 7

Santiago varsity football defeated Elsinore 31–14 in the CIF Southern Section Division 8 first round at Santiago High School in Corona. MaxPreps and the Southern Section scoreboard list the result as final. The victory sends the Sharks to Friday’s quarterfinal against Santa Monica, again at Santiago. The published bracket had Santiago as the No. 3 seed, and the Sharks are now among eight teams left in the division.

Norco boys water polo defeated La Serna 12–8 in Friday’s Division III quarterfinal. MaxPreps, the CIF bracket and the Los Angeles Times all confirm the score. The Cougars advanced to a semifinal against Troy at 5 p.m. Tuesday, November 10; MaxPreps labels the contest neutral, while the NFHS listing places it in Fullerton. Because neither feed names the pool, families should confirm the exact facility before traveling.

Long Beach Poly defeated Norco 39–17 in the Division 3 football first round at Norco High School. The result closes a season in which the Cougars earned a home playoff game after competing through the Big West schedule. Long Beach Poly advances to meet Edison. The official page confirmed the matchups, kickoff times and fields but had not posted final scores Monday morning. Corona teams were listed in the boys 19U final against Moreno Valley and the girls 19U final against Menifee. Those matches remain schedule confirmations, not reported results.

## Around the Community

The City of Norco is accepting coed youth basketball registration through November 16. The league serves ages 4–13 and costs $115 for Norco residents or $125 for nonresidents. Practices begin November 30, with Saturday games starting January 9 at Riley Gymnasium. Volunteer coaches are still important to team placement.

Corona’s Circle City winter youth basketball registration continues through Friday, November 13 for ages 4–14. The city program guide lists mandatory skills testing that day and practices beginning the week of January 4, 2027. Families should confirm remaining space through Corona Recreation Services.

Corona-Norco NJB held its coed and high-school evaluations Sunday at Riley’s Gym. Its next published evaluation date is Sunday, November 15, for the girls-only league in grades three through eight. The league says registration remains open only while division space is available.
