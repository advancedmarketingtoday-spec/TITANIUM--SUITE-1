# Corona Sports Today: Norco Water Polo Plays for the Final and Centennial Volleyball Visits Murrieta Valley

Two local teams step into important playoff matches Tuesday. Norco boys water polo meets Troy in the CIF Southern Section Division III semifinals, while Centennial girls volleyball visits Murrieta Valley in the CIF State Championships. Both contests offer another chance for Corona-area families to cheer on teams that have already built strong seasons.

## Last Night's Results

No Monday final involving Centennial, Corona, Santiago, Norco or Eleanor Roosevelt was posted in the public feeds reviewed by 6:31 a.m. Tuesday. Norco’s varsity and JV girls water-polo teams were scheduled at Temescal Canyon, at 4 p.m. and 5 p.m., but the MaxPreps pages still showed preview listings rather than final scores. Those games remain unverified and are not being reported as wins, losses or cancellations.

The most recent confirmed school final remains Norco boys water polo’s 12–8 victory over La Serna in Friday’s Division III quarterfinal. That result followed playoff wins over Burbank, 14–9, and Camarillo, 12–10. The Cougars’ public team page lists a 23–8 overall record and an 8–2 league mark, good for second in league play. Tuesday’s semifinal determines one place in Saturday’s championship at Mt. San Antonio College.

Santiago football’s 31–14 win over Elsinore also remains the latest confirmed football result among the five schools. The Sharks are 7–4 overall and advance to host Santa Monica in the Division 8 quarterfinals Friday. Centennial did not play last weekend because its Division 1 playoff matchup begins this Friday at Mater Dei.

## Around the Community

Corona’s Circle City winter youth basketball registration continues through Friday, November 13, for ages 4–14. The city program guide lists mandatory skills testing that day and practices beginning the week of January 4, 2027. Families should confirm space through Corona Recreation Services before attending.

The City of Norco is accepting coed youth basketball registration through Monday, November 16. The league serves ages 4–13 and costs $115 for residents or $125 for nonresidents. Practices begin November 30, and Saturday games are scheduled to start January 9 at Riley Gymnasium. The city notes that volunteer coaches are important because teams without one may be waitlisted.

Corona-Norco NJB lists girls-only evaluations for grades three through eight on Sunday, November 15. Registration remains open only where divisions have space. Families should consult the league page for the current division count, evaluation time and any waitlist instructions rather than relying on an older availability number.

Good luck to Norco and Centennial in Tuesday’s playoff matches, and to every local athlete preparing for the rest of the week. Corona Shoutouts is cheering for the players, coaches, officials, volunteers and families who bring care and good sportsmanship to each pool, court and field.
