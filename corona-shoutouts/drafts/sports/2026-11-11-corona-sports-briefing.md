# Corona Sports Today: Centennial goes five sets; Norco’s playoff run ends in the semifinals

Centennial girls volleyball stretched its state-playoff opener to five sets Tuesday night, while Norco boys water polo finished a deep CIF Southern Section run in the Division III semifinals. The final scores brought two strong postseason journeys to a close, and the local calendar now turns toward Thursday water polo and two Friday football playoff games.

## Last Night's Results

Centennial girls volleyball battled Murrieta Valley through all five sets before the Nighthawks won 3–2 at Murrieta Valley High School. Centennial took the second and third sets, 25–20 and 25–23, and forced extra points in the fourth before Murrieta Valley won 27–25 and then took the deciding set 15–12. MaxPreps listed the full set sequence as 25–20, 20–25, 23–25, 27–25, 15–12. The result ended Centennial’s run in the 2026 CIF State Girls Volleyball Championships, but the Huskies made the host team earn every step of the advance.

Norco boys water polo closed its CIF Southern Section Division III playoff run with a 17–14 semifinal loss to Troy. MaxPreps recorded the game as a Troy home result in Fullerton, while its matchup page had previously labeled the contest neutral; the exact pool was not identified in the public listing. Norco reached the semifinal after three playoff wins and finished the MaxPreps schedule at 23–9 overall, including an 8–2 Big VIII record. Troy advanced to Saturday’s Division III final at Mt.
