# Corona Sports Today: Centennial stuns Mater Dei; Santiago rolls into the semifinals

Centennial and Santiago gave the Corona area two memorable football victories Friday night. The Huskies defeated Mater Dei 45–15 at Santa Ana Stadium, while the Sharks beat Santa Monica 52–21 at home; both teams now move into CIF Southern Section semifinal games next Friday.

## Last Night's Results

Centennial defeated Mater Dei 45–15 on Friday, November 13, at Santa Ana Stadium in a CIF Southern Section Division 1 quarterfinal. MaxPreps reported that the Huskies built a 21–0 lead in the first nine minutes and that Jaden Jefferson accounted for five touchdowns. The result extended Centennial’s winning streak to six games and moved the Huskies to 8–3 overall after a 5–0 Big West–Upper League season. Centennial is scheduled to host Sierra Canyon in the Division 1 semifinals at 7 p.m. Friday, November 20.

Santiago defeated Santa Monica 52–21 on Friday at Santiago High School in a CIF-SS Division 8 quarterfinal. MaxPreps identified 52 points as Santiago’s highest total of the season, an especially bright note in the Sharks’ second straight playoff win. The victory advanced Santiago to a semifinal against Rancho Verde at 7 p.m. Friday, November 20. The public schedule did not clearly identify next Friday’s venue, so families should confirm before traveling.

No other Friday final involving the five covered schools was confirmed through the public pages reviewed by the reporting cutoff. That is a reporting limitation, not a statement that no other teams competed. Several lower-level and individual-event feeds did not post complete results overnight.

## Players and Teams to Know

Centennial’s full football team earns the first Saturday shoutout. Scoring 45 points against Mater Dei and advancing from the Division 1 quarterfinals is a major team achievement, and the opening 21–0 run reported by MaxPreps gave the Huskies control early. Jefferson’s five-touchdown contribution was the standout individual line published in the MaxPreps game report.

Santiago’s football team also deserves a community salute. The Sharks followed their first-round playoff win with a season-high 52 points, according to MaxPreps, and secured another week together.
