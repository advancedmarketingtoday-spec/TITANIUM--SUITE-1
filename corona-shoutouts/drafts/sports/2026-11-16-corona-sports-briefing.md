### Weekend recap — Friday, November 13

Centennial defeated Mater Dei 45–15 at Santa Ana Stadium in a CIF Southern Section Division 1 quarterfinal. MaxPreps reported that the Huskies led 21–0 within the opening nine minutes and that publicly named quarterback Jaden Jefferson accounted for five touchdowns. Centennial improved to 8–3, extended its winning streak to six games and advanced to host Sierra Canyon at 7 p.m. Friday, November 20.

Santiago defeated Santa Monica 52–21 at Santiago High School in a CIF-SS Division 8 quarterfinal. The 52 points were Santiago’s highest total of the season, according to MaxPreps, and the result lifted the Sharks to 8–4. Santiago now travels to Rancho Verde for a semifinal at 7 p.m. Friday, November 20.

## Players and Teams to Know
