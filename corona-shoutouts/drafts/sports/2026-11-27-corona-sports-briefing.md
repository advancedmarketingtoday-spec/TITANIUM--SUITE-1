# Corona Sports Today: Centennial plays for a title, SilverLakes showcase opens

Centennial football takes the area’s biggest stage tonight, meeting St. John Bosco for the CIF Southern Section Division 1 championship at the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum. Closer to home, Centennial and Eleanor Roosevelt basketball join the BattleZone showcase at Centennial High, while hundreds of youth soccer teams begin a three-day weekend at SilverLakes.

## Players and Teams to Know

Centennial football receives today’s first team shoutout. The Huskies are 9–3 on the published MaxPreps schedule and reached the Division 1 championship by beating Mater Dei 45–15 and Sierra Canyon 42–19 in the previous two playoff rounds. Those verified results set up tonight’s meeting with 9–3 St. John Bosco; no outcome or title claim is being made before the game is played.

Centennial varsity boys basketball enters the BattleZone at 4–0. The Huskies have verified wins over Bakersfield Christian, Norte Vista, Rancho Christian and Victory Christian Academy. Their Friday opponent, Sunnyslope of Phoenix, is listed at 1–0, making the 7 p.m. home matchup a meeting of two unbeaten teams.

Eleanor Roosevelt varsity boys basketball also arrives at Centennial High with a perfect early record. The Mustangs opened 1–0 after a 90–57 win over Beaumont and face Hesperia at 2:30 p.m. The result will be a tournament game, not a Big VIII League contest, so it will affect the overall record without deciding a league position.

## Eastvale Soccer

The SilverLakes Fall Showcase begins today at 8 a.m. at SilverLakes in Norco and continues through Sunday, Nov. 29. The organizer lists boys and girls U13–U19 divisions, with all games at the 132-acre complex. Teams must check in at least 30 minutes before their first match, daily parking is listed at $25 per car and $50 per RV, and pets are not allowed except properly identified service animals.

The schedule includes actual team kickoffs throughout the day, separate from the 8 a.m. event start. Public schedule data shows Empire Surf ECNL RL B2009/10 playing Apple Valley SC at 10 a.m. on Field 19, and Empire Surf ECNL RL B2007/09 playing LA International FC at 11:50 a.m., also on Field 19. “Empire Surf” should not be confused with Eastvale’s Empire Soccer Club; no Empire Soccer Club team assignment was confirmed in the public schedule reviewed for this draft.

Eleanor Roosevelt’s boys and girls high school soccer teams did not show a Friday match with a complete opponent, kickoff and venue. The next clearly listed local school soccer block is Monday at Centennial, where the Huskies’ freshman, JV and varsity boys are scheduled to host Foothill.

Eastvale families planning for spring can still use Empire Soccer’s published Spring 2027 recreational registration. The club lists birth years 2008–2023, a current late fee of $205 plus $10 for non-JCSD residents, and its next walk-up session on Saturday, Dec. 5, from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. at Eastvale Community Park, 12750 Citrus Street.

AYSO Region 37 has its next calendar milestone on Dec. 5 for 10U, 12U and 14U area playoffs. The Area 1R schedule lists play at Heritage Lake Sports Park in Menifee on Dec. 5–6, including Corona 10U boys and girls pool matches. Those December assignments are verified upcoming events, not part of this weekend’s SilverLakes showcase.

## Around the Community

Friday offers three very different ways to support local sports: a championship football trip to the Coliseum, a full basketball showcase at Centennial and a major youth soccer weekend at SilverLakes. Families should allow extra travel time and use the venue’s current ticket, parking and field information before leaving home.
