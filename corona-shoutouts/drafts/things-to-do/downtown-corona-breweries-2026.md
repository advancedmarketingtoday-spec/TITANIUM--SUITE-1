# Downtown Corona / Breweries: Your Guide to Main Street Brewery and Beyond

**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

## Quick Answer

Downtown Corona's brewery scene centers on Main Street Brewery, a working brewpub at 493 N Main St that's been pouring house-made beer since 1997, plus Storytellers Brewery and Meet House a few blocks away on N Maple St. If you're coming from Riverside, you can hit both in an evening, and nearby options like Stone Church Brewing and Karl Strauss round out a short Corona brewery crawl.

- **Main Street Brewery** is a 10-barrel house brewery co-located with Lamppost Pizza, still brewing on site at 493 N Main St, Corona.

## What Breweries Are in Downtown Corona?

Downtown Corona's core brewery lineup is small but distinct: Main Street Brewery on N Main St and Storytellers Brewery and Meet House on N Maple St, both within about a half-mile of the Corona Civic Center.

Main Street Brewery sits directly in the heart of downtown — it practically *is* downtown, having relocated across the street after the Corona Plaza redevelopment in 2017. Storytellers Brewery is about a five-minute walk or two-minute drive away, making both walkable in a single evening. If you're driving in from Riverside, plan for roughly 15–20 minutes down the 91 Freeway.

**Decision rule:** Choose Main Street Brewery for a historic, pizza-paired brewpub feel; choose Storytellers for a newer "meet house" social-hall vibe.

## Main Street Brewery Corona: Hours, Location, and Reviews

Main Street Brewery is located at 493 N Main St, Corona, CA 92878; phone (951) 371-1471. It operates as a brewpub co-located with Lamppost Pizza and has been a Corona fixture since 1997.

Reviews consistently describe it as a 10-barrel house brewery pouring around 12 house beers alongside roughly six rotating guest taps — enough variety for a group with mixed tastes.

**Common mistake:** Don't assume this is a newer craft startup. Reviewers often rate it highest on nostalgia and consistency, not experimental flavors.

## Corona Brewery Tours, Tastings, and Weekend Events

Corona's breweries don't run formal guided tours the way large regional breweries do, but tastings and flights are standard practice.

- **Main Street Brewery:** flights available on request; brewing equipment visible from the dining area.
- **Storytellers Brewery:** regularly hosts live music, trivia nights, and small gatherings.
- **Weekend events:** check each brewery's social pages — live music and themed nights rotate weekly.

**Edge case:** For a group tasting of six or more, call ahead — small taprooms fill up fast on Friday and Saturday evenings.

## Best Craft Beers at Downtown Corona Breweries

Main Street Brewery's standout lineup includes the Hop Daddy IPA, Bishop's Tipple Trippel, and Honey Blonde Lager — a hoppy option, a Belgian-style strong ale, and an easy-drinking session beer.

Storytellers Brewery and nearby Stone Church Brewing round out the local beer map with their own house styles, so a two-stop crawl covers IPA, lager, and specialty ale territory without repeating flavors.

## Happy Hour Specials and Budget-Friendly Visits

Yes — you can absolutely visit Corona breweries on a budget. Taproom pints, flights, and shared appetizers keep most visits well under a full-service restaurant.

- Arrive during early evening weekday hours for the best happy hour pricing.
- Split a flight with a friend instead of ordering two full pints.
- Pair Lamppost Pizza with a pint special to stretch your budget further.

## Food, Food Trucks, and Family-Friendly Vibes

Most downtown Corona breweries serve food on-site rather than relying on food trucks. Main Street Brewery's Lamppost Pizza partnership means a full pizza menu is always available alongside the beer.

Family-friendliness varies by time of day: early evenings tend to be casual and welcoming to kids, while later hours skew toward an adults-only crowd. If you're bringing children, aim for an early dinner slot and confirm seating policy by phone first.

**Mistake to avoid:** Don't assume every brewery allows kids after dark — some taprooms restrict minors past a certain hour for licensing reasons.

## What Makes Corona (California) Beer Different From Corona Beer?

If you're searching "Corona beer," you're likely thinking of the Mexican import lager — a completely different product from Corona, California's local craft breweries. Local Corona breweries brew small-batch beer on site, which means fresher, more varied styles than a mass-market import.

Local breweries emphasize house recipes, rotating guest taps, and brewpub food pairings — a distinctly different experience from grabbing a bottled import at a bar.

## Outdoor Seating at Corona Breweries

Several downtown-area breweries offer outdoor or patio seating, making them solid picks for mild Southern California evenings.

- **Best for groups:** outdoor patio seating at Storytellers for live music nights.
- **Best for quick bites:** sidewalk-adjacent tables near Main Street Brewery's pizza counter.

**Edge case:** Patio availability can shrink during peak weekend hours.
