# Glen Ivy Hot Springs: A Relaxed Spa Day If You'd Rather Unwind Than Hike

**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

## Quick Answer

Glen Ivy Hot Springs is a 12-acre adults-focused day spa resort in Temescal Valley, near Corona, California, built around naturally mineral-rich hot spring water and a signature red clay mud bath called Club Mud. If you live in Corona and want a low-effort, no-hiking-boots-required escape, Glen Ivy gives you 19 pools and water features, spa treatments, and quiet lounge space — all included with general admission.

## Key Takeaways

- **Location:** 25000 Glen Ivy Road, Temescal Valley, near Corona, roughly 60 miles from Los Angeles
- **What it is:** A historic mineral springs resort dating to 1860
- **Core experience:** 19 pools and water features plus Club Mud, California's only red clay mud bath
- **Vibe:** Adults-only, relaxation-first — no trails, no gear, no sweat required
- **Admission:** Day passes and memberships available, pricing varies by season and day
- **Best for:** Anyone wanting a soak-and-lounge day instead of an active outdoor adventure

## What Is Glen Ivy Hot Springs?

Glen Ivy Hot Springs is a historic mineral springs resort that's been welcoming guests since 1860, making it one of the oldest wellness destinations in Southern California. The property sits on about 12 landscaped acres near the Cleveland National Forest, but the grounds are gardens, pools, and lounge areas — not hiking trails. That's the whole point: it's designed for guests who want to sit still, soak, and let a spa day do the work.

## Location, Hours, and How Far It Is From Los Angeles

Glen Ivy is located at 25000 Glen Ivy Road in Temescal Valley, directly between Corona and the Cleveland National Forest foothills. It's roughly 60 miles from Los Angeles and about an hour inland from Newport Beach.

For Corona residents specifically, Glen Ivy is essentially a local resort — a short drive rather than a planned road trip. Official hours and seasonal schedule changes are listed on Glen Ivy's "Plan Your Day" page.

## Prices, Admission, and Membership vs Day Pass

General admission includes access to all 19 pools and water features. Beyond the day pass, Glen Ivy offers membership options for guests who plan to visit regularly.

- **Choose a day pass if** you're visiting once or occasionally.
- **Choose a membership if** you live nearby and want a regular wellness routine.

Exact pricing tiers and package details are maintained on Glen Ivy's official Admission Packages page.

## Amenities and Facilities

- 19 pools and water features (included with general admission)
- Club Mud — California's only red clay mineral mud bath
- Spa treatment rooms for massages and facials
- Lounge areas and gardens across the 12-acre property
- On-site dining options

## What to Bring, Dress Code, and Rules

Pack light and practical: a swimsuit, a cover-up or robe, flip-flops, and a reusable water bottle.

- Swimsuit (required for pools and Club Mud)
- Flip-flops or water sandals
- Sunscreen (much of the property is outdoors)
- A change of clothes for after your mud rinse
- ID and payment method for add-on treatments

## Is Glen Ivy Hot Springs Worth It?

Glen Ivy is worth it if your priority is relaxation over activity and you value a unique, naturally sourced spa experience. The combination of included pool access, the Club Mud ritual, and a genuinely old resort heritage gives it a different feel than a standard day spa. It may be less worth it if you're looking for a high-energy family outing or a budget option.

## Best Time to Visit

Weekday mornings tend to offer a quieter, more relaxed experience, since day spas see heavier traffic on weekends. Mild spring and fall weather makes for the most comfortable soaking conditions; summer visits reward guests who arrive early. Always confirm current hours on the official site before planning.

## Parking and Accessibility

Glen Ivy provides on-site parking for day guests, and the resort layout is designed around easy movement between pools, lounges, and treatment areas. The grounds are level and easier to navigate than a trail-based destination. Check the resort map ahead of time to plan your route.

## Can You Bring Kids?

Glen Ivy is primarily marketed and designed as an adults-only day spa retreat. If you're planning a family outing with children, confirm current age policies directly with Glen Ivy before booking.
