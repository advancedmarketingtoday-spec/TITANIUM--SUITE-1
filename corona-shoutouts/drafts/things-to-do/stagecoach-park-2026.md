# Stagecoach Park Corona: Your Complete Guide to Hours, Amenities, and Fun

**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

## Quick Answer

Stagecoach Park Corona is a free, city-run neighborhood park at 2125 Stagecoach Road in Corona, California, open daily from 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. It features an Old West-themed playground, covered picnic shelters, BBQ grills, restrooms, and walking paths across roughly 8–9 acres. There's no entrance fee, and it's a solid pick for families, dog walkers, and anyone wanting a quiet spot to stretch their legs.

## Key Takeaways

- **Location:** 2125 Stagecoach Road, Corona, CA 92880, managed by the City of Corona
- **Hours:** Open daily, roughly 5 a.m. to 10 p.m.
- **Cost:** Free to enter; no admission fee for general park use
- **Size:** About 8–9 acres with ADA-accessible sidewalks
- **Playground:** Old West theme with separate structures for different age groups
- **Amenities:** Picnic areas, BBQ grills, covered shelters, restrooms, drinking fountains, bike racks
- **Walking path:** Flat, stroller- and wheelchair-friendly loop, longest stretch about 0.4 miles
- **Contact:** City of Corona Parks & Facilities at 951-736-2241

## What Is Stagecoach Park Corona?

Stagecoach Park Corona is a city-owned neighborhood park designed for everyday outdoor recreation rather than large-scale events. The park was originally planned as a neighborhood sports park and includes a parking lot, a 300-foot ballfield, two playground structures, and connecting sidewalks. What makes it stand out is the Old West theme woven into the playground design.

**Choose Stagecoach Park if** you want a low-key, well-maintained space for a picnic or playtime without crowds or event fees.

## Where Is Stagecoach Park Located?

Stagecoach Park sits at 2125 Stagecoach Road, Corona, CA 92880, in a residential part of the city near Corydon Street. Plug the address directly into your GPS; many nearby residents walk or bike over rather than drive.

## Hours and Admission

Stagecoach Park is open daily from approximately 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. There's no admission fee for general grounds use. The 10 p.m. closing time is later than many neighborhood parks, so evening visits are fine.

## How Much Does It Cost?

Standard use — picnicking, playground time, walking — costs nothing. The only potential costs come from optional add-ons like reserving the pavilion for a private event. Call 951-736-2241 for reservation fees and availability.

## Stagecoach Park vs Other Parks Nearby

Stagecoach Park is best described as a neighborhood park rather than a destination park. Choose it for a calm, kid-friendly space close to home; choose a larger regional park for sports fields, large venues, or extensive trail networks.

## Best Things to Do

- Play on the Old West-themed structures (separate zones for younger kids and ages 5–12)
- Pack a picnic under one of the covered shelters with BBQ grills nearby
- Walk the flat, stroller-friendly loop path
- Host a small gathering in the pavilion (includes electricity)
- Bring the dog for a casual stroll

## Reviews and Ratings

Reviewers consistently describe Stagecoach Park as clean, peaceful, and family-friendly, with praise for the playground variety and shaded seating. Common themes include clean bathrooms, good variety of play equipment, and the unique Old West playground theme.

## Is It Good for Kids?

Yes. The age-separated playgrounds (ages 5–12 plus a separate area for younger kids) suit toddlers and older children alike, and the flat paved paths make it easy to push a stroller.

## Parking and Directions

There's an on-site parking lot near the main entrance off Stagecoach Road. Sidewalks connect the park to nearby Corydon Street. Use 2125 Stagecoach Road, Corona, CA 92880, in your map app, and arrive early on weekends if you're planning a picnic.

## Dog-Friendly / Pet Policy

Specific leash rules aren't detailed in the city's general amenities listing, so the standard city-wide leash rule typically applies. Always check posted signage on-site.

## Picnic Areas and Trails

The picnic areas include covered, shaded shelters with nearby BBQ grills, and the walking trail is a flat loop suitable for strollers and wheelchairs. Grab a shaded table early, bring your own charcoal for the grills, and combine a picnic with a walk for a simple half-day outing.
